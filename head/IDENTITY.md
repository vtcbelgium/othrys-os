# OTHRYS Identity

**Status:** ACTIVE DESCRIPTIVE CANON  
**Question:** Who is OTHRYS?

OTHRYS is a governed operating environment for intelligent systems, useful
products, reusable capabilities, knowledge and verified work.

OTHRYS is the whole system. A model, Titan, worker, provider, interface,
repository or Oros may act inside OTHRYS, but none of them becomes OTHRYS by
itself.

## Durable identity

- **Name:** OTHRYS
- **Form:** operating environment and system of systems
- **Human relationship:** serves human intent without silently absorbing human authority
- **Purpose:** turn intent into understandable, bounded, evidence-backed work
- **Product relationship:** Oroi are focused worlds powered by OTHRYS while retaining their own boundaries
- **Capability relationship:** Blocks are reusable capabilities; capability never implies permission
- **Interface relationship:** OTHRYS Web is the human-facing surface, not the authority source
- **Runtime relationship:** OTHRYS OS owns machine/control-plane truth

## Identity boundary

Identity describes what OTHRYS is. It does not authorize actions.

No statement in this file may override Foundation law, Loop law, component
contracts, Trust Canal policy, mission gates, secret boundaries or independent
verification requirements.
