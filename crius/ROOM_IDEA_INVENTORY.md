# CRIUS ROOM IDEA INVENTORY

> Status: CANONICAL IDEA RESERVOIR — room-by-room opportunity inventory. This is discovery material, not implementation proof and not permission to monetize.
> Owner: CRIUS / House of Crius
> Mission: keep every commercial room stocked with concrete, testable opportunities connected to OTHRYS assets and capabilities.

## 0. Operating rule

Every room should contain three kinds of ideas:

1. **Near-term extractable** — can be produced largely from capabilities OTHRYS already has or is actively building.
2. **Composable** — can be assembled from reusable OTHRYS organs, Buddy Core, Mnem, Prometheus, Forge, Evidence Gate, Builder Arena, Switchyard, Atlas, Themis, Talos, Keymaster and related infrastructure.
3. **Frontier** — ambitious bets that justify research because they could become major differentiators.

Every idea should eventually be scored for pain, frequency, payer, proofability, build cost, support burden, distribution, margin, recurrence, defensibility, legal/compliance burden and strategic learning.

## 43. FINAL HOUSE RULE

CRIUS should never ask only:

> “Can we sell this?”

He should ask:

> “What value does this create, who economically benefits, what evidence proves it, what commercial mechanisms exist, which mechanism preserves trust best, and what reusable asset do we gain even if this specific product never earns money?”

The House is full only when every room contains concrete hypotheses, evidence queues and kill criteria — not when it contains a long list of slogans.
