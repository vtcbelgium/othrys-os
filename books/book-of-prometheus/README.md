# The Book of Prometheus

## Purpose
Intelligence and evidence evaluation over explicit, provenance-bound capability and research evidence.

## Current house law
Prometheus discovers, scores, evaluates and recommends. The resident kernel is pure and deterministic: it consumes explicit capability/evidence facts and emits explainable intelligence or digest-bound evidence artifacts. It owns no orchestration, credentials, scheduling, knowledge admission, engineering execution, verification, or lifecycle authority.

Freshness is not fitness. Evidence grade is not authority. A recommendation is evidence for a decision, never the decision itself. Below-floor quality cannot be compensated by cheapness or openness, and future-dated evidence is suspect rather than privileged.
