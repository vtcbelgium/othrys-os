# The Book of Mnemosyne

**Status:** OTHRYS OS housekeeping edition — evidence-bound, non-authoritative by itself.

## Purpose
Knowledge governance and institutional memory over explicit evidence.

## Current house law
Mnemosyne admits, classifies, relates, searches, reviews and preserves; it also exposes currentness, bounded context, logical knowledge-zone read models and deterministic evidence transport capsules. Context Metabolism acts only after selection: project truth stays pinned, reconstructible estate evidence may travel by digest reference, and required/authority evidence plus frozen identities cannot be lost or expanded. Rebuildable term-hit caches are catalog-digest keyed and may accelerate exact lexical recall but never become source truth. **The Great Harvest** is Mnemosyne's permanent non-authoritative quarry over recoverable code lineage, commit intent, and fingerprinted live-only/uncommitted stock: source payload remains in Git, historical objects remain explicitly historical, and relevant prior stock must be consulted before new capability design. Mnemosyne does not research, build, execute, or silently promote.

## Book rule
This Book may be expanded only from inspectable implementation, tests, mission results, receipts, ADRs, or reviewed Mnemosyne evidence. Contradictions are recorded; history is never silently rewritten.

## Operational memory / self-honing
During V2 bring-up, bounded diagnostics and self-honing runs automatically append secret-free operational events to Mnemosyne's local archive under `.othrys/knowledge/archive/operations/`. PASS, DEGRADED and FAIL observations are retained with actor, job, evidence summary and lesson. FAIL events are marked review-recommended but are not silently promoted into canonical knowledge.

This operational archive is deliberately reconstructible, local and non-authoritative. It exists so troubleshooting history is not lost while the Pentarchy learns how to build and verify itself. Canonical promotion still requires the normal Mnemosyne review law.
