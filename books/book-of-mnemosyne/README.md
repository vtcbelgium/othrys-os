**Status:** OTHRYS OS housekeeping edition — evidence-bound, non-authoritative by itself.

## Purpose
Knowledge governance and institutional memory over explicit evidence.

## Current house law
Mnemosyne admits, classifies, relates, searches, reviews and preserves; it also exposes currentness, bounded context, logical knowledge-zone read models and deterministic evidence transport capsules. Context Metabolism acts only after selection: project truth stays pinned, reconstructible estate evidence may travel by digest reference, and required/authority evidence plus frozen identities cannot be lost or expanded. Rebuildable term-hit caches are catalog-digest keyed and may accelerate exact lexical recall but never become source truth. **The Great Harvest** is Mnemosyne's permanent non-authoritative quarry over recoverable code lineage, commit intent, and fingerprinted live-only/uncommitted stock: source payload remains in Git, historical objects remain explicitly historical, and relevant prior stock must be consulted before new capability design. Mnemosyne does not research, build, execute, log every event, or silently promote.

## Book rule
This Book may be expanded only from inspectable implementation, tests, mission results, receipts, ADRs, or reviewed Mnemosyne evidence. Contradictions are recorded; history is never silently rewritten.

## Operational events and self-honing

Raw operational events belong to the canonical OTHRYS event ledger under `.othrys/logs/`, not to Mnemosyne knowledge. The canonical envelope is `othrys.os.event.v1`; failures or recurring patterns may be marked review-recommended without becoming knowledge.

When an operational event contains a durable lesson, Mnemosyne may capture a bounded, sourced summary into `.othrys/knowledge/inbox/`. Normal review law then decides whether that summary is promoted, rejected or parked.

Historical operational material already stored under `.othrys/knowledge/archive/operations/` remains preserved as compatibility evidence. New writers must not extend that historical location.

The compatibility API `runtime/os/mnemosyne_operations.mjs` now routes operational writes through `runtime/os/event_ledger.mjs` so existing callers do not need a flag-day migration.
