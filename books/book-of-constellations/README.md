# The Book of Constellations and Stars

**Status:** CANONICAL HOUSE MAP — governed intelligence projection.

## Definition
A **Constellation is not the business and not the runtime product.** It is OTHRYS' governed observation, organisation and management projection over authoritative reality.

There are two scopes:
- **Great Constellation** — OTHRYS-wide institutional/portfolio projection.
- **Micro-Constellation** — exactly one bounded projection for one Oros.

## Stars
A **Star** is a managed operational capability domain inside a Micro-Constellation: e.g. Commerce, Analytics, Security, Communication, Marketing or Finance. A Micro-Constellation may hold `0..N` Stars.

A Star is not a Block. Blocks build product capability; Stars observe/manage capability domains after composition. One Star may observe several Blocks, Bridges and bespoke Oros components. One Block may emit signals useful to more than one Star without changing ownership.

## Authority
The Oros remains authoritative runtime reality. A Constellation derives evidence and recommendations; it cannot rewrite the Oros simply because its projection disagrees. Titans belong to OTHRYS and may operate/maintain Stars only through governed authority.

## Data flow
`Oros evidence -> Micro-Constellation -> Stars -> appropriate Titans -> institutional learning / recommendations`

Cross-Oros learning may contribute to the Great Constellation, Mnemosyne and the Great Library, but tenant/product boundaries remain intact.

## Current implementation truth
The Oros/Constellation model is ratified historical law. Do not claim a complete Star lifecycle runtime merely because the model exists. Current OTHRYS OS should expose only what implementation and evidence prove.
