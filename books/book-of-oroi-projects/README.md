# The Book of Oroi

**Status:** CANONICAL HOUSE MAP — product-world definition.

## Definition
An **Oros** is one sovereign governed digital world allocated to one product, customer, organisation or individual. **Oroi** is the plural. A generic development folder may be a project; it becomes an Oros only when OTHRYS gives it an Oros identity, canonical Blueprint and governed lifecycle.

## The Oros owns reality
The Oros is authoritative for its running product reality: product/business identity, user-facing applications and websites, domain data, product-specific services, selected integrations, product history and operational lifecycle.

Every Oros has **exactly one canonical Blueprint**. The Blueprint describes desired capability and constraints; it is not runtime truth. A future `oros.lock` records the exact composition resolved by Factory; the lock is a receipt, not authority.

## Composition
An Oros may contain:
- eligible canonical Blocks;
- exact Bridges/provider adapters;
- explicit product adapters/glue;
- product-specific code where reuse is not justified.

The Oros must not receive forked hidden copies of canonical Blocks. Product-specific behavior belongs in declared configuration/adapters or stays product-specific.

## Governance relationship
OTHRYS OS governs and supports an Oros without becoming it. Each Oros receives one bounded Micro-Constellation for observation/management. Titans belong to OTHRYS OS, not to the Oros.

## Rule
Do not use `Oros` as a synonym for UI, repository, app, Block, Star, mission or workspace. Those are different species.

## Current house law
This Book synthesizes product-world law and **grants no authority**. Oros identity, execution, composition and release still require their canonical Mission, Trust and verification evidence.
