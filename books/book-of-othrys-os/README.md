# The Book of OTHRYS OS

**Status:** CANONICAL HOUSE MAP — subordinate to Foundation Laws and ratified constitutional evidence.

## Definition
OTHRYS OS is the **control plane and operating environment**. It is the system that understands, plans, builds, verifies, operates, repairs, learns from and governs digital product worlds. It is not itself an Oros and never becomes a customer's product or business.

## Owns
OTHRYS OS owns control-plane concerns: Missions and Work, governance and authority, Titans, Factory, Hephaestus, Talos, Mnemosyne, Atlas, Prometheus, Rhea, Kronos, Keymaster, Hermes, Switchyard, Mycelium, operator surfaces, institutional knowledge, training and evidence.

## Does not own
OTHRYS OS does not own a product's business data, user-facing product identity or domain reality. Those belong to an Oros. It does not own reusable product implementation that has earned Block status; canonical Block source belongs in `othrys-blocks`.

## Core hierarchy
`OTHRYS OS -> Great Constellation -> Titans -> governed Oroi -> Blueprint -> Factory composition -> Oros runtime reality -> Micro-Constellation -> Stars`

Blocks and Bridges are **composition inputs**, not children in the authority hierarchy. They are selected into an Oros through its Blueprint/Factory path.

No subsystem may silently become an Oros, grant itself authority, or redefine these species. History remains provenance; current house definitions live here and in the hierarchy map.

## Current house law
This Book is a map of current evidence and **grants no authority**. Runtime code, accepted law, Mission/Trust gates and Talos evidence remain authoritative within their declared boundaries.
