# The Book of Theia

**Status:** CANONICAL ACTIVE TITAN — creative/media production and authored perception.  
**Titan:** Theia  
**Control plane:** Opsis  
**Canonical domain root:** `theia/**`

## Definition

Theia is the OTHRYS Titan of **creative form**: original intellectual property, story, visual development, audiovisual production, animation, media packaging, property continuity, and the production evidence that connects an idea to a finished master.

Theia does not exist merely to generate media. She exists to make creative work **repeatable without becoming generic**.

> **THEIA CREATES FORM.**

## Scope

Theia owns the creative-production horizon:

`IDEA -> PROPERTY/CANON -> STORY -> SCRIPT -> VISUAL PLAN -> STORYBOARD -> ANIMATIC -> PRODUCTION -> MASTER -> DERIVATIVES -> RELEASE PACKAGE -> AUDIENCE EVIDENCE -> CREATIVE LEARNING`

Current major internal surfaces:

- **Opsis** — Theia's production control plane and artifact/dependency graph.
- **Story Forge** — narrative intelligence, Story Genes, property bibles, continuity and script contracts.
- **Media Factory** — production, assembly, packaging, localization and derivative manufacture.
- **Cartoon Production** — specialized scripted-animation doctrine.
- **Properties** — persistent creative worlds such as Pemberton.
- **Research stock** — inspiration/research harvested under originality and rights firewalls.

Animation is one capability of Theia, not the definition of Theia.

## Opsis

Opsis tracks:

`PROPERTY -> SEASON -> EPISODE -> SCENE -> BEAT -> SHOT -> ASSET -> VERSION -> RECEIPT`

Opsis knows what exists, what depends on what, what is locked, what is draft, what requires review, and which production artifact is current.

Opsis is not a governance bypass. Human locks, Trust Canal, Talos verification, rights controls and external authority boundaries still apply.

## Canon / Working / Fossil

Theia keeps property-local creative state explicit:

- **CANON** — operator-approved creative truth.
- **WORKING** — mutable development state.
- **FOSSIL** — rejected ideas plus rejection reason.

Mnemosyne remains OTHRYS institutional memory. Theia owns working creative state and property canon; durable cross-house knowledge is admitted through Mnemosyne rather than duplicated.

## Boundary with Hyperion

Hyperion and Theia are sibling Titans with different questions.

**Hyperion asks:** Is this worth pursuing, what is the audience/economic opportunity, and should it scale, pause, pivot, license, monetize or die?

**Theia asks:** What is this property, what story are we telling, is the character on-model, does the script work, what should the audience see/hear, and which master is canonical?

> **HYPERION DECIDES WHETHER THE BET DESERVES OXYGEN. THEIA DECIDES HOW THE WORK TAKES FORM.**

Hyperion retains Prospector, Fishing Fleet, Distributor, Laboratory, Oracle, Magnifier, Merchant, Valve, Portfolio and Vault.

Story Forge and Media Factory belong to Theia.

Distributor remains Hyperion because distribution strategy, audience acquisition and reach allocation are economic/discovery functions. Theia produces truthful platform-native packages; Hyperion decides where and how hard to distribute them.

Vault remains Hyperion's transferable-asset/IP-economic preservation surface. Theia owns production provenance and working creative canon; Vault preserves commercial/transferable asset state.

- **Rhea** interprets vitality and repair needs.

- **Visual Control** owns operational visual observation/evidence; Theia owns authored visual/media creation.

## Human authority

Human locks remain appropriate for property canon, character identity, season/story direction, script lock, animatic lock, master lock and publish approval.

> **AUTOMATE REPETITION. PRESERVE CHARACTER. TEST STORY BEFORE RENDER. KEEP RIGHTS. KEEP RECEIPTS. LET HUMANS KEEP TASTE.**
