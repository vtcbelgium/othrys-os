## Ownership
- Kronos owns the daily cadence evidence, not the research content.
- Prometheus owns discovery, news, opportunity description and gap hunting.
- Keymaster owns credential custody, presence/health and sealed use.
- Talos owns qualification evidence; discovery is never certification.
- Mnemosyne keeps report/intake lineage and learned facts.
- Hermes owns delivery contracts when the report leaves the OS.
- Rhea watches vitality; Switchyard may consume only qualified sanitized readiness.

The operator remains the acquisition gate. `ADD` starts intake; it never means auto-enable or paid consent.
## Newsletter law
Maximum eight items: up to four AI, two tech and two Syntra-watch items. The Syntra lens is grounded in the current Study Buddy programme: **Full stack & AI automation developer**. Daily web search is three Tavily BASIC queries, no generated answer, no raw-content fetch, no paid fallback: nominally three credits/day.

An item becomes actionable only after Prometheus has enough structured facts to identify it as an API/model/tool/source opportunity. Action cards expose exactly `ADD` and `DENY`.

`DENY` is inert. `ADD` checks Keymaster first. If account/credential setup is missing, intake stops and prompts the operator. If custody is available, a read-only basic probe runs, then Keymaster/Talos receive a qualification request. Passing the basic probe still does not enable the resource.
