# Official Blocks, Oroi and Constellations

**Status:** harvest-derived architecture reconciliation; evidence-bound, non-authoritative by itself.  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Senior sources:** Hub `BOOK_OF_BLOCKS.md`, `BLOCK_COMPOSITION_LAW.md`, ADR-0050/0051, Oros Venture Doctrine, V2 admission evidence.

## The key correction
OTHRYS currently has two useful things called "Blocks" that must not be confused. Level 1/2 produced many tiny, excellent tested primitives; the older canonical Book of Blocks reserves official **Capability Block** for a user-meaningful reusable product capability. Both inventories are valuable, but their species differ.

A slugifier, checksum, canonical-JSON function, retry formula, atomic JSON file or schema checker is normally a **Primitive / Operation / Pattern**. Search, collections, messaging, gamification, booking, marketplace, notifications, analytics instrumentation, media preparation or affiliate monetisation may qualify as **Capability Blocks** when their boundary and evidence satisfy doctrine.

Do not throw the training shelf away. It is the subsoil from which official Blocks can be assembled cheaply and verified strongly. The correction is classification, not deletion.

## Proposed layered shelf
`Primitive -> Pattern -> Capability Candidate -> RAW/PROVEN Block specimen -> REUSABLE official Block -> CERTIFIED -> GOLDEN`

- **Primitive:** deterministic bounded operation; excellent direct reuse, no product-maturity claim.
- **Pattern:** proven composition/law such as atomic replace, proposal-only write, append-only transition ledger, or one-door boundary.
- **Capability Candidate:** user-meaningful product outcome found in a real Oros/quarry.
- **Block specimen:** canonical packaged capability with Passport, provenance, tests and explicit Ports/boundaries.
- **REUSABLE official Block:** same canonical version succeeds in origin and an independent second Oros, under operator-approved promotion.
- **CERTIFIED/GOLDEN:** increasingly repeated cross-product/version/edge evidence. Golden remains hard to earn.
## Who owns what

| Layer | Owns | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| OTHRYS | governance, Titans, Factory, institutional memory, verification, routing | the customer's product/business |
| Hephaestus | quarry, contract/extraction work, repair and maintenance under mission authority | promoter or Oros owner |
| Talos | independent Block and composition verification | source of product intent |
| Great Library | evidence/provenance shelf and reusable knowledge | automatic authority |
| Blueprint | desired Oros capabilities, outcomes, necessity, constraints, reuse policy and acceptable ranges | exact runtime resolver |
| Factory | exact eligible Block/Bridge selection and composed-product verification | maturity author |
| Oros | sovereign operational product reality | a copied folder or Constellation projection |
| Micro-Constellation | bounded observation/management projection over one Oros | builder or runtime truth |
| Star | managed operational capability domain over an Oros | Block identity |
| Mycelium | bounded movement of compatible evidence, claims and artifacts | permission, product owner or resolver |
| Operator | promotion/admission and other reserved gates | an inferred automated approval |

## The composition chain

`Venture Thesis -> Blueprint capability need -> Factory resolution -> exact Blocks + Bridges + product glue -> Oros runtime -> evidence -> Micro-Constellation / Stars -> Titans -> learning -> quarry/refinement`

A future `oros.lock` is the receipt between Blueprint desire and Oros reality: exact Block IDs/versions/digests, Bridges, adapters, config-reference names and evidence IDs. It records; it does not rule.

The fundamental reusable asset is not just the Block. It is the **Block + proven integration edge + consumer evidence**. Each successful composition should make later Oros resolution cheaper and safer.
## Block-to-Block connections

Connections are typed contracts, not folder imports by accident. Each official Block Passport should eventually declare `provides`, `requires`, `optional`, data ownership, side effects, trust assumptions and absence behaviour. A required edge must resolve to a named compatible Block, product adapter or Bridge, or composition stops with an explicit gap.

Examples: collection valuation can require `collection.records` plus `market.price.insight`; price alerts can require a watched-price source and optionally notification delivery; marketplace listings can optionally connect to messaging and promotion credits without owning either; product SEO can consume catalog publication truth without owning the catalog database.

This prevents mega-Blocks. The marketplace Block does not absorb messaging, wallet, image upload and moderation simply because one product uses them together. Those become explicit composition edges and therefore reusable portfolio evidence.

## Block-to-Star connections

A **Star is not a package**. A Star is the Oros-level managed domain that may observe and govern zero, one or many Blocks, Bridges, product-specific modules and external services. Example: a Commerce Star could watch marketplace listings, affiliate offers, price insight and a payment Bridge. An Analytics Star could watch visit tracking, product events and Oros KPIs.

The Star consumes signals and health from those components; it does not confer Block maturity or replace Block contracts. One Block can emit evidence useful to more than one Star while remaining owned once. Star topology is therefore an operational projection over composition, not another dependency graph pretending to be runtime truth.

## Great Constellation

Each Oros has one bounded micro-Constellation. OTHRYS' Great Constellation can later aggregate governed cross-Oros evidence such as Block/version adoption, failing edges, cost, health, traction and reusable lessons. It must preserve tenant/Oros boundaries and provenance. It learns that an edge worked; it does not silently install that edge elsewhere.

This gives the portfolio loop its useful shape: repeated Oros compositions strengthen or weaken evidence about Blocks and edges, while Hephaestus/Talos and operator gates remain responsible for actual change and promotion.
## Current official/reuse posture

The canonical implementation home remains sibling repo `othrys-blocks`. V2 admission evidence currently has three admitted capability identities: image preparation, affiliate offers and visit tracking. The first two have `REUSABLE` admission evidence; visit tracking remains `RAW`. Therefore normal composition eligibility is presently narrow by design.

The larger V2 training shelf is a high-value **Forge stockroom**, not 70+ automatically official product Blocks. Its primitives can power Capability Blocks and make their Talos contracts much stronger. No rename/migration is performed by this reconciliation document.

## Promotion proof worth collecting

For each Capability Candidate, collect: real origin caller; frozen outcome/boundary; exact canonical package digest; origin reintegration; independent negative controls; second-Oros integration using the same source; Oros-level acceptance; security/privacy proportional to authority; exact dependency/Bridge edges; rollback; known limitations; operator decision.

For higher maturity, accumulate multiple real compositions, version-upgrade evidence, edge compatibility, failure/recovery history, maintenance ownership and measurable product value. Do not optimize for Block count. Optimize for **portable capability × useful consumers × verified edges × product leverage**.

## Immediate architectural consequence

Level 3 should become the first serious composition laboratory: applications should preferentially assemble the L1/L2 primitive stock, while separately identifying which user-meaningful bundles deserve future Capability-Block contracts. This teaches OTHRYS both directions at once: bottom-up reuse of primitives and top-down discovery of true product capabilities.

No Level 3 activation, Block promotion, Constellation runtime or automatic resolver is authorized by this document.
