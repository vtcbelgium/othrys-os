## What the organism learned

**Hephaestus** now has task-specific evidence about first-pass construction, no-mutation repair failure, destructive repair regression and when a heavier smith actually helps. Raw worker success is explicitly not semantic success.

**Talos** caught genuine code defects, destructive repairs and transport regressions, but Level 1 also exposed verifier mistakes. Oracle corrections are preserved as verifier-learning evidence rather than rewriting history.

**Rhea** gains failure/recovery sequences: defect streaks, failed repairs, successful recoveries, regressions and operator-intervention debt. These are vitality signals, not merely build logs.

**Mycelium** gains route experience: task family, builder/model, device, duration, resource state where present, retries and verified outcome. Trail strength may inform future recommendations but never permission.

**Mnemosyne** keeps provenance, stock/reuse decisions, final artifacts, failure classes and lessons. **Kronos** receives sequence/timing evidence. **Switchyard** receives route/outcome evidence. **Prometheus** can use recurring gaps to guide future technology scouting without admitting anything automatically.

## Important patterns

Qwen3.5 9B repeatedly proved useful as a fast local first builder, including clean first-pass wins, but also produced many no-mutation repair attempts. Qwen3-Coder 30B sometimes rescued bounded repairs, but also produced destructive regressions such as lost exports or broken module structure. The correct conclusion is task-specific fitness, not a global winner.

Operator intervention was used only after governed repair routes failed and is recorded as **capability debt**, never builder credit. Transport failures, BOM/encoding mistakes, malformed request paths and missing receipts are part of the dataset because orchestration quality matters as much as model quality.

## Governance conclusion

Level 1 proves repeatable atomic construction and verification under real friction. It does **not** prove autonomous construction, Block admission, or readiness for a higher level by itself. The next curriculum transition must be explicit and governed.
