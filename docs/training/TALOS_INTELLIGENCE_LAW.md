# Talos Intelligence Law

**State:** ACTIVE FOUNDATION
**Purpose:** turn verified execution evidence into bounded system adaptation, not passive logging.

Talos is the central verification-and-learning nexus of the training chain. Builders, routing, timing, recovery, resource use and application behavior may generate observations, but only independently verified evidence may become learning input.

The learning loop is:

`OBSERVE -> TALOS VERIFY -> SYNTHESIZE -> CROSS-ORGAN ADAPT -> RE-VERIFY`

Logging is evidence preservation, not learning. Learning exists only when verified evidence changes a future recommendation, ranking, diagnostic signal, timeout hint, research question, oracle coverage decision or knowledge index.

Talos never becomes execution authority. It may make the system smarter; it may not mint permission.## Organ adaptations

- **Hephaestus:** consume verified first-pass rate, recovery rate and latency as builder-quality evidence.
- **Switchyard:** prefer routes with proven low recovery cost inside the existing legal/authority policy.
- **Kronos:** learn bounded timeout classes from measured execution time and timeout evidence.
- **Rhea:** receive repeated no-mutation, timeout and recovery streaks as care/diagnostic signals.
- **Mnemosyne:** index verified lessons, failure classes and provenance instead of raw success claims.
- **Prometheus:** turn repeated verified gaps into focused research questions rather than generic scanning.
- **Mycelium:** learn successful model/device/latency trails as placement evidence.
- **Talos:** expand its own oracle coverage where defects repeatedly survive the builder's first pass.

Adaptations that alter authority, admission, paid usage, production state, Trust Canal policy or level progression remain operator-gated. Bounded quality/routing evidence may be consumed automatically because it changes preference, not permission.
