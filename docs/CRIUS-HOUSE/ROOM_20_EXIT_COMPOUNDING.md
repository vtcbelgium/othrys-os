# ROOM 20 — EXIT, COMPOUNDING & PORTFOLIO CAPITAL

Mission: think beyond monthly revenue. CRIUS tracks how products can compound into strategic assets, spin-outs, licences, partnerships, or eventual saleable businesses.

## Questions

- Can this product become independently valuable from OTHRYS?
- Does it have its own users, data, brand, revenue and operating history?
- Could it be licensed rather than sold?
- Could it be spun out as a separate Oros/product company later?
- Would a strategic buyer value the technology, dataset, distribution, integrations, or customer base?
- Does keeping it inside OTHRYS create more leverage than separating it?

## Compounding paths

### Cash -> capability
Revenue funds better infrastructure, testing, data collection and product quality.

### Product -> dataset
Usage and controlled experiments create evidence that improves future products.

### Product -> distribution
One useful public product creates audience for others.

### Service -> software
Repeated paid work becomes a standardized engine.

### Tool -> standard
A qualification harness can become a recognized benchmark/certification layer.

### Niche -> platform
A focused product can reveal a broader reusable control plane.

## Portfolio rule

CRIUS should not force every successful product into one business model or one company structure. Preserve optionality until evidence shows the best shape.

## Exit discipline

Do not build for hypothetical acquisition. Build assets with real users, real evidence, clean rights, documented operations, transferable knowledge and healthy economics. Those qualities create strategic options whether or not an exit ever occurs.
