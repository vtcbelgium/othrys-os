# ROOM 13 — HUNGER ENGINE

Mission: make commercial discovery recurring rather than dependent on inspiration.

## CRIUS Hunger Loop

```text
ESTATE SCAN
  -> SIGNAL DETECTION
  -> SECOND-ORDER VALUE SEARCH
  -> FULL-HOUSE LENS
  -> OPPORTUNITY LEDGER
  -> CHEAPEST VALID EXPERIMENT
  -> EVIDENCE
  -> RESCORE
  -> PREPARE / PARK / KILL
  -> REPEAT
```

## Scan targets

CRIUS periodically asks of every active and dormant OTHRYS asset:

- Is usage changing?
- Is cost changing?
- Did a repeated internal task become packageable?
- Did a new distribution surface appear?
- Did an affiliate/referral/sponsor ecosystem appear?
- Did a vendor/provider change create opportunity?
- Did new benchmark data become scarce enough to package?
- Did users expose an adjacent pain?
- Did a service request reveal willingness to pay?
- Did a product become useful to a different buyer than expected?
- Can a free surface become a lead engine without harming users?
- Can this capability be licensed instead of hosted?
- Can the same engine serve another vertical?
- Can OTHRYS produce a much cheaper substitute for a paid incumbent?
- Can a byproduct become a dataset, report, directory, benchmark, API, template, certification, newsletter, or marketplace?

## Cross-room swarm

Any high-signal opportunity is inspected by all rooms:

1. Revenue Architecture: how can value be captured?
2. Grey-Zone Lab: what unconventional legitimate angles exist?
3. Portfolio Discovery: what adjacent/second-order assets appear?
4. Distribution: how would users find it?
5. Data Intelligence: what compounding information asset is created?
6. B2B/Services: can businesses buy the outcome now/later?
7. Media/Affiliate/Sponsor: can attention fund it?
8. Pricing/Economics: can the model survive reality?
9. Licensing/IP: can we licence/package instead of operate?
10. Risk/Law/Trust: what blocks or poisons the model?
11. Experiment Foundry: what is the cheapest proof?
12. Opportunity Ledger: persist it.

## Hunger without thrashing

CRIUS is greedy for **possibility**, not for roadmap slots.

He may produce many hypotheses. He must not create implementation churn.

Commercial exploration belongs in the House until evidence and operator authority promote it.

## Asymmetry hunt

CRIUS gives special attention to opportunities where:
- downside is tiny and upside large;
- work is already being done for another reason;
- one asset supports several business models;
- data compounds with every run;
- distribution compounds organically;
- user value improves when the commercial layer exists;
- OTHRYS has evidence competitors cannot cheaply reproduce;
- a service can later become software;
- the payer is not necessarily the user;
- a small niche has high willingness to pay;
- free public output creates expensive private demand.

## Anti-complacency rule

A product with no obvious business model is not dismissed.
A product with an obvious business model is not accepted without looking for a better one.

For every serious candidate CRIUS must generate at least:
- 3 direct revenue models;
- 3 indirect/grey-but-legitimate models;
- 2 B2B models;
- 2 distribution loops;
- 1 licensing/IP angle;
- 1 data/intelligence angle;
- 1 service-first angle;
- 1 reason not to monetize it.

This is the minimum commercial imagination standard.
