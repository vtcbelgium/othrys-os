# ROOM 14 — EXTERNAL SCOUTING

Mission: investigate the outside world for commercial patterns, pricing, competitors, affiliate/referral programs, regulations, market gaps, distribution surfaces, and adjacent opportunities that could materially change CRIUS scoring.

## Scope

This room does not grant implementation or commercial authority. It gathers current external evidence and feeds the other rooms.

## Watch domains

### Market gaps
- poor incumbent UX
- expensive manual services
- fragmented tool stacks
- recurring complaints
- communities repeatedly asking for the same workaround
- new platform/API capabilities that make an old problem newly cheap to solve

### Pricing and packaging
- competitor pricing
- free-tier limits
- enterprise packaging
- usage metrics
- hidden cost drivers
- support/SLA differences

### Distribution surfaces
- app/plugin marketplaces
- affiliate programs
- reseller programs
- partner directories
- developer ecosystems
- educational channels
- newsletters/directories
- procurement marketplaces

### Vendor economics
- AI provider pricing
- hosting costs
- model changes
- outages
- API terms
- referral incentives
- volume discounts

### Commercial rule changes
- Belgian/EU rules relevant to operator status and product monetization
- consumer rules
- privacy/AI regulation
- platform policy changes
- affiliate/sponsorship disclosure requirements

### Competitive intelligence
- what products exist
- what customers complain about
- what is commoditized
- what remains expensive
- what differentiators appear defensible

## Scouting output

Every external finding must record:
- date
- source
- claim
- affected CRIUS opportunity
- confidence
- likely shelf life
- whether it changes score, risk, economics, or experiment design

## Anti-copy law

External products are quarry for patterns, pricing, unmet needs, and lessons. They are not permission to copy protected code, branding, datasets, or proprietary material.

## Trigger

A high-potential opportunity should not receive a serious Commercial Opportunity Packet until this room has checked the current external market where freshness is material.
