# ROOM 01 — REVENUE ARCHITECTURE

Mission: map every legitimate way OTHRYS-created value can turn into revenue, economic leverage, or strategic advantage.

## Core principle

Never ask only: `What can we charge for?`
Ask:

- Who receives measurable value?
- Who has budget?
- Who benefits if the product stays free?
- Who pays to reduce risk, save time, increase output, gain insight, gain distribution, or gain access?
- Can the payer be different from the user?
- Can one asset support several revenue channels without corrupting the product?

## Every product review must answer

1. Primary user.
2. Economic buyer.
3. Core value event.
4. Measurable value created.
5. Direct revenue options.
6. Indirect revenue options.
7. Third-party-funded options.
8. B2B options.
9. IP/licensing options.
10. Data/intelligence options.
11. Service wrapper options.
12. Revenue channels that would damage trust.

15. Best fallback model if the preferred model fails.
