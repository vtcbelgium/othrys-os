## Packaging models

### Developer licence
Small libraries, SDKs, schemas, policy packs, templates.

### Commercial-use licence
Free/non-commercial experimentation; paid commercial use only where licensing structure and code provenance support it.

### Enterprise licence
Self-hosted deployment, updates, policy packs, support and governance.

### OEM / embedded
Another product embeds an OTHRYS engine or component under contract.

### White-label
Customer/agency presents the front end; OTHRYS provides the underlying engine.

## IP hygiene gate

Before licensing:
- provenance of code/content is known
- third-party licence obligations are mapped
- copied/harvested material is legally separable from original OTHRYS IP
- trademark/name issues are checked
- training/data source rights are understood
- customer-specific material is excluded

PandaOS harvesting or other external reference work is knowledge/research stock unless rights clearly permit direct redistribution. CRIUS may monetize OTHRYS's independently implemented know-how, not someone else's protected code merely because it was studied.

## Strategic question

For every internal component ask:

> Is the higher-value business running this component for users, or letting other businesses build on it?

Sometimes infrastructure earns more as a component than as a product.
