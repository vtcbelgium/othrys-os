# ROOM 17 — PARTNERSHIPS & CHANNELS

Mission: identify cases where OTHRYS gains more by partnering than by acquiring every customer alone.

## Partner types

- agencies
- consultants

- hosting/cloud/AI providers
- software vendors

- dealers/marketplaces

## High-interest OTHRYS pairings

### Agencies
OTHRYS provides diagnostics, private benchmarks, reports, implementation machinery; agency owns relationship and sales.

### Training providers
Study Buddy or AI apprenticeship tooling can be institutionally packaged while students receive a good free/low-cost experience.

### AI/hosting providers
Potential sponsor/referral/technical partnerships around benchmarks or optimization, with strict editorial independence.

### Universities/research
Public evals, agent qualification, reproducibility and benchmark collaboration can create credibility, datasets and future contracts.

### Collectors/dealers
Collector OS could integrate discovery/listings/valuation evidence while clearly separating commercial listings from provenance/valuation logic.

## Partner test

Before pursuing a partner:
- what unique asset do they bring?
- what do we bring?
- who owns customer relationship?
- who owns data?
- who supports the user?
- who carries liability?
- can they influence rankings/evidence?
- is revenue dependency too concentrated?
- can the relationship be exited cleanly?

## Rule

Partnership must amplify distribution, trust, capability, or economics. Do not accept a partner merely because they offer money.
