# ROOM 10 — RISK, LAW & TRUST

Mission: keep CRIUS commercially aggressive without becoming reckless.

## Rule

Commercial uncertainty is classified, not hand-waved.

States:
`CLEAR | REVIEW_NEEDED | BLOCKED | PROHIBITED`

`UNKNOWN` is never silently converted to `CLEAR`.

## Review domains

- course/training restrictions
- unemployment/benefit implications
- self-employment status
- business registration
- invoicing ability
- social contributions
- tax/VAT obligations

### Consumer obligations
- pricing transparency
- refund/cancellation
- digital service obligations
- warranties where applicable
- recurring billing clarity
- complaint/support path

### Privacy/data
- lawful basis
- consent where required
- telemetry
- retention
- cross-border processors
- sensitive data
- children's/student data
- data-processing agreements
- deletion/export rights where applicable

### Commercial disclosure

### Platform/terms

### High-risk product domains

## Trust risks beyond law

A tactic can be lawful and still strategically stupid.

Red flags:
- monetization meaningfully worsens core UX
- rankings influenced by payout
- opaque data resale
- addiction/dark patterns
- impossible promises
- misleading scarcity
- fake urgency
- lock-in without export
- surprise billing
- exploiting user mistakes

## CRIUS trust question

Before opening a channel ask:

> If the exact commercial mechanism were displayed prominently to the user, would we still be comfortable defending it?

If no, redesign it.

## Course Mode

During Course Mode this room maintains a living checklist of what must be legally/administratively confirmed before the global financial lock can be opened. Product readiness and operator permission remain separate evidence streams.
