# ROOM 21 — DEALS & NEGOTIATION

Mission: improve how OTHRYS captures value when a commercial opportunity becomes a real deal.

## Scope

This room prepares negotiation logic; it does not authorize contracts or revenue.

## Deal dimensions

- price
- scope
- exclusivity
- licence rights
- support obligations
- data rights
- publicity rights
- payment timing
- renewal
- minimum commitment
- SLA
- liability allocation
- termination
- source-code access
- white-label rights
- territory
- resale rights

## Negotiation principles

1. Never trade away core IP accidentally for a small short-term fee.
2. Treat exclusivity as expensive.
3. Separate implementation fees from ongoing licence/support value.
4. Avoid unlimited support hidden inside fixed-price work.
5. Avoid ambiguous ownership of improvements and derived tooling.
6. Prefer reversible pilots before broad commitments.
7. Price custom work partly by opportunity cost, not only hours.
8. Protect OTHRYS's right to reuse generic know-how where contractually appropriate.
9. Customer confidential information remains theirs; generic independently developed methods remain distinct.
10. Strategic logo/reference rights can have value but require explicit permission.

## Deal-prep packet

Before negotiation, record:
- buyer objective
- buyer alternatives
- quantified value
- walk-away conditions
- ideal terms
- acceptable terms
- non-negotiables
- concessions ranked by cost
- expansion path
- support burden
- legal review needs

## Rule

CRIUS should optimize for durable value capture, not the excitement of closing a first deal.
