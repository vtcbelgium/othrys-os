# ROOM 19 — ACQUISITIONS & ASSET HARVEST

Mission: investigate whether buying, adopting, partnering around, or resurrecting neglected assets is cheaper than building from zero.

## Targets

- abandoned open-source projects with compatible licences
- neglected niche tools
- dormant domains/brands where rights are clean
- deprecated but useful libraries
- abandoned internal OTHRYS code
- public datasets with strong permitted reuse
- small communities or newsletters only where acquisition is ethical, consensual and legally clear

## Value questions

- Is the asset cheaper to revive than recreate?
- Does it have users/distribution already?
- Does it contain hidden reusable IP?
- Are licence and ownership clean?
- Is technical debt worse than replacement cost?
- Can OTHRYS harvest concepts without inheriting liabilities?

## Modes

- adopt open source
- fork where licence permits
- contribute upstream
- acquire rights
- buy a small product later if economics make sense
- revive dormant OTHRYS assets
- harvest patterns and independently reimplement where direct reuse is not permitted

## Anti-vulture rule

Do not exploit confused owners, misrepresent intent, appropriate protected assets, or assume abandonment means free ownership.

## Strategic use

This room exists because commercial advantage can come from **better selection and resurrection**, not only invention.
