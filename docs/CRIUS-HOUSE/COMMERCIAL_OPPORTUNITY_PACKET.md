## Minimum imagination test

No packet is complete until CRIUS has considered at least:
- 3 direct revenue models
- 3 indirect/grey-but-legitimate models
- 2 B2B models
- 2 distribution loops
- 1 licensing/IP angle
- 1 data/intelligence angle
- 1 service-first route
- 1 reason monetization could damage the product

## Decision rule

A packet is analysis, not authority. It may recommend `GATE_ELIGIBLE`; only the operator may authorize opening under the Book of Crius.
