## Brain decision contract

Each admitted Web command receives one persistent `othrys.os.brain-decision.v1` record.

It binds:
- original command digest;
- Jev observation digest;
- deterministic front-door intent;
- FAST/LIGHT/DEEP lane;
- executor identity/class/owner;
- mission requirement;
- authorization requirement;
- verification profile;
- bridge receipt digest;
- source: `JEV_CORTEX` or `DETERMINISTIC_FALLBACK`.

Every decision remains:
- `authorityGranted=false`;
- `actionApplied=false`;
- `executionStarted=false`.

## Deterministic safety floors

Jev cannot downgrade an action that the deterministic front door already considers consequential.

A mission is required when either:
- deterministic intent is PLAN or BUILD; or
- semantic lane is DEEP; or
- Jev says execution is required; or
- task type is admin/build; or
- risk >= 2.

Therefore a bad semantic classification cannot turn a deterministic build into a no-mission request.
