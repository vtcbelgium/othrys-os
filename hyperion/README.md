## Frugal First law

> **FRUGAL FIRST. ALWAYS.**

Cost is an architectural variable. Hyperion does not default to the strongest or most expensive tool. OTHRYS seeks the cheapest credible route that meets the required verified outcome and escalates only when evidence justifies it.

Canonical search order:

`DO NOTHING -> REUSE -> CACHE -> DETERMINISTIC -> LOCAL -> OWNED/IDLE -> OPEN SOURCE -> LEGITIMATE FREE -> CHEAP PAID -> SPECIALIST -> PREMIUM`

The objective is not zero spend. It is minimum total resource cost per verified useful outcome, including money, tokens, compute, latency, energy, human attention, maintenance, failure and lock-in.

A premium route must keep earning its place. Prometheus may discover challengers; Tool Olympics benchmarks them; Switchyard routes; Keymaster tracks provider/key health and quotas; Hyperion judges economics.

## Anti-slop law

Cheap generation is not value. Before a candidate becomes public, Hyperion asks whether it deserves to exist. Reject generic wrappers, no-distribution products, support traps and worse copies of incumbents.

A candidate should beat an incumbent on at least one meaningful dimension and preferably several: speed, price, friction, privacy, portability, batch processing, evidence, automation, specialization, reliability or distribution.

## Anti-slip law

No product earns future capital because past capital was spent.

> **IF THIS DID NOT EXIST TODAY, WOULD WE CHOOSE TO SPEND THE NEXT HOUR, EURO, TOKEN, DEPLOYMENT SLOT OR SUPPORT MINUTE ON IT?**

A tiny autonomous positive asset may survive. A larger asset may die when support, maintenance, risk and opportunity cost exceed value.
