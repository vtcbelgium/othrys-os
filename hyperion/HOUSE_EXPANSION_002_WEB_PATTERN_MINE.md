## Room O — ASSET FLIP / SPINOUT
Candidates that achieve independent traction can become standalone properties.

Build transferable qualities from the start:
- isolated domain/product identity when justified
- independent analytics
- clean code/IP boundaries
- reproducible deploy
- low operator dependence
- clean cost/revenue ledger
- user/retention history
- documented operations

Then evaluate KEEP / BUNDLE / LICENSE / SPIN OUT / SELL.

## Room P — FAILURE MINE
Study dead/abandoned products too.

Questions:
- useful job but bad distribution?
- wrong pricing?
- support burden?
- API/platform killed it?
- no recurrence?
- too generic?
- technically fragile?
- founder fatigue?
- incumbents copied feature?

Failed products can reveal jobs worth rebuilding with a different cost structure.
