# HYPERION LOW-TOUCH MARKET SCAN 001

> Status: COMMERCIAL RESEARCH — not implementation proof and not permission to monetize.
> Filter: build-once / sell-many / self-serve. Exclude customer-heavy consulting, onboarding, retainers, bespoke delivery, and high-touch support as primary business model.
> Date: 2026-09-08

## 1. OPERATOR CONSTRAINT

Primary commercial preference:

> **MINIMIZE CUSTOMER INTERACTION.**

Hyperion should prefer products that can be discovered, purchased, installed, run, upgraded, and supported with minimal direct human involvement.

Default priority order:

1. self-serve software;
2. digital packs/templates/workflows;
3. paid libraries/vaults;
4. usage-priced automations/agents;
5. APIs/data/benchmark products;
6. affiliate/distribution revenue attached to self-serve products;
7. asynchronous support only where necessary;
8. customer-specific service only as an optional exception, not the core engine.

## 4. WHAT APPEARS TO SELL

Based only on publicly visible “Most Popular” labels, install counts, listed usage and product positioning — not private revenue data — recurring winners fall into these classes:

1. **Workflow libraries / vaults.** Buyers want many reusable automations in one purchase/subscription.
2. **Single-purpose automation templates.** Clear result beats broad capability.
3. **Content transformation.** One input -> multiple publishable outputs.
4. **Lead follow-up / CRM routing.** Fast, measurable business outcome.
5. **Reporting / summarization.** Weekly reports, board reports, extraction and routing.
6. **Monitoring / competitive intelligence.** Pricing changes, costs, errors, alerts.
7. **Domain kits.** Preconfigured commands/agents for a specific role or problem.
8. **One-click snapshots / complete setups.** Buyers pay to skip configuration.
9. **Pay-per-run agents.** No setup and no recurring commitment.
10. **Full libraries with constant additions.** Subscription becomes justified by continuous new inventory.

## 5. HYPERION FILTER FOR OTHRYS

Score commercial candidates with a new Low-Touch multiplier.

High score if:
- instant checkout;
- instant delivery;
- no sales call;
- no onboarding call;
- user can configure from documentation;
- no tenant-specific code;
- automatic billing;
- automatic upgrades;
- automated diagnostics;
- self-service cancellation/export;
- usage is measurable automatically;
- support can be handled primarily by docs/AI/community;
- product improves from aggregate execution evidence.

Penalty if:
- discovery calls required;
- bespoke integration required;
- recurring meetings;
- custom client reporting;
- manual fulfillment;
- customer-dependent scope;
- support burden scales linearly with users.

Strongest forms:

`DOWNLOADABLE ASSET`

`ONE-CLICK INSTALL`

`SELF-SERVE SAAS`

`PAY-PER-RUN`

`API`

`DATA / INTELLIGENCE SUBSCRIPTION`

`MARKETPLACE`

Avoid making “book a call” the normal path.

## 8. SUPPORT LAW

Low-touch does not mean low quality.

Every OTHRYS commercializable product should aim for:

`DOCS -> AUTOMATED PREFLIGHT -> INSTALL -> SELF TEST -> EVIDENCE -> AUTO DIAGNOSIS -> FAQ/AI SUPPORT -> HUMAN ONLY AS ESCALATION`

If a product repeatedly requires human explanation, installation or repair, Hyperion should treat that as product debt rather than an acceptable sales process.
