# BOOK OF MEDIA FACTORY

**Hyperion Arm of Media Production, Packaging, and Derivative Manufacturing**

Media Factory converts approved research, stories and product knowledge into finished native media artifacts. Story Forge decides narrative structure; Media Factory manufactures the artifact.

> **ONE CANONICAL IDEA. MANY NATIVE FORMS. ZERO EXCUSE FOR SLOP.**

## 1. Mission
`SOURCE OBJECT -> FORMAT CONTRACT -> STORY/SCRIPT INPUT -> SCENE PLAN -> ASSETS -> ASSEMBLY -> QA -> PLATFORM PACKAGE -> HANDOFF`

## 2. Canonical Source
Every derivative points to canonical research/story/product evidence. Shorts, long video, article, carousel, quiz and printable may share truth while using different pacing and composition.

## 3. Production Blocks
Storyboard/shot planner; asset router; illustration/animation; voice; music/SFX rights; editor/assembler; subtitles; localization; thumbnail/title lab; metadata packager; provenance ledger; renderer; quality gate.

## 4. Native Format Law
Do not blindly resize. A Short needs immediate compression; long-form needs breathing room; a carousel needs visual sequence; children's media needs age-appropriate density; technical media needs inspectable evidence.

## 5. Production Contract
Declare format, audience, duration, aspect, source version, story version, visual identity, voice, factual sensitivity, rights, target quality, cost budget and review level.

## 6. Asset Provenance
Track generated/commissioned/licensed assets, model/tool, source material, music, fonts, voice rights and transformations where material. Unknown rights fail closed for publication.

## 7. Quality Gates
Factual accuracy; continuity; broken frames; visual artifacts; audio intelligibility; sync; subtitles; spelling; age suitability; rights; disclosure; repetitive content; metadata; CTA/link validity.

## 8. Production Economics
Measure research handoff friction, asset cost, render cost, human review, rework, time/finished minute, localization cost and derivative marginal cost. Seek minimum sufficient quality, then invest more only when evidence justifies it.

## 9. Template Intelligence
Templates preserve useful production mechanics without making every artifact identical. Version templates and record which properties use them.

## 10. Autonomy
`DRAFT -> HUMAN_APPROVAL -> APPROVED_LOW_RISK_TEMPLATE -> BOUNDED_AUTO_PRODUCTION -> EXCEPTION_REVIEW`
Publication authority remains separately governed.

## 11. Anti-Slop
Reject generic voice+stock filler, random visual changes, factual footage mismatches, copied creator aesthetics, low-quality translation, repetitive mass output and artifacts whose production trick is more interesting than their value.

## 12. Laws
> **ONE CANONICAL IDEA. MANY NATIVE FORMS.**

> **THE CALENDAR DOES NOT OUTRANK THE QUALITY GATE.**

> **AUTOMATION SHOULD LOWER THE COST OF CARE, NOT REMOVE CARE.**
