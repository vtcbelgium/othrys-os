# BOOK OF LABORATORY

**Hyperion Arm of Experiment Design, Causal Learning, and Evidence Discipline**

Laboratory turns activity into learning. It prevents OTHRYS from mistaking random variation, anecdotes and lucky spikes for knowledge.

> **CHANGE WITH A QUESTION. MEASURE WITH A CONTRACT.**

## 1. Mission
`HYPOTHESIS -> BASELINE -> VARIABLE -> TEST -> OBSERVE -> ANALYZE -> DECIDE -> MEMORY`

## 2. Experiment Contract
State hypothesis, target population/context, success metric, guardrails, variable, baseline/control when feasible, sample/time budget, cost ceiling, stopping rule and decision outcomes.

## 3. Experiment Types
A/B; sequential bounded batches; prototype usability; pricing simulation; channel test; hook/thumbnail; format; localization; feature; workflow; model/tool bakeoff; demand probe; retention cohort.

## 4. Causal Humility
Not every real-world experiment permits clean causality. Label evidence strength. Correlation may guide another test but should not masquerade as proof.

## 5. Media Testing — Theia Interface
Possible variables: topic, hook, title, thumbnail, duration, narrator, visual style, story genome, posting surface, language and CTA.

Laboratory owns the **experiment contract**:
- hypothesis;
- variable;
- population/context;
- budget;
- success/failure evidence;
- stopping rule.

Theia owns production of the media variants and media-domain quality/rights gates.

Laboratory must not demand a "cleaner" experiment by weakening Theia's safety, rights, audience-classification or disclosure rules.

## 6. Product Testing
Activation, task completion, return use, failure rate, time-to-value, support burden, willingness-to-pay signals, performance and cost.

## 7. Guardrails
Experiments may not weaken safety, privacy, rights, factual standards or financial gates merely to obtain cleaner data.

## 8. Small Samples
Do not overfit tiny datasets. Record uncertainty. A viral outlier is a reason to investigate, not a universal law.

## 9. Experiment Ledger
Preserve hypothesis, version, dates, inputs, outputs, metrics, anomalies, interpretation, decision and follow-up. Negative results are valuable if trustworthy.

## 10. Laws
> **CHANGE WITH A QUESTION. MEASURE WITH A CONTRACT.**

> **NO HYPOTHESIS, NO EXPERIMENT. NO SIGNAL, NO SCALE.**

> **ONE SPIKE IS AN EVENT. REPEATABILITY IS EVIDENCE.**

> **FAILED HYPOTHESES ARE ASSETS WHEN THE TEST WAS GOOD.**
