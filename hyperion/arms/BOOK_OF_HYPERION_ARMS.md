# BOOK OF HYPERION ARMS

**Canonical architecture for Hyperion's specialized capability arms**

Hyperion remains one Titan. The Arms are not new Titans and do not compete with OTHRYS governance. They are stable capability families through which Hyperion performs economic discovery, creative formation, experimentation, distribution, interpretation, scaling, commercialization and preservation.

> **ONE TITAN. MANY ARMS. ONE VALUE LOOP.**

## 1. The Twelve Arms

1. **Prospector** — Opportunity Discovery and Candidate Formation.
2. **Story Forge** — Story Intelligence and Original IP Formation.
3. **Fishing Fleet** — Rapid Product Experiments and Tiny Useful Products.
4. **Media Factory** — Media Production and Derivative Manufacturing.
5. **Distributor** — Discovery, Reach, Routing and Owned Audience.
6. **Laboratory** — Experiment Design and Causal Learning.
7. **Oracle** — Signal Interpretation and Winner Detection.
8. **Magnifier** — Winner Expansion and Compounding.
9. **Merchant** — Commercial Intelligence and Revenue Design.
10. **Valve** — Financial Gates and Revenue Airlocks.
11. **Portfolio** — Resource Allocation and Bet Management.
12. **Vault** — IP, Rights, Provenance and Transferable Asset Memory.

## 2. Primary Flow

`PROMETHEUS -> PROSPECTOR -> [STORY FORGE | FISHING FLEET | OTHER BUILD PATH] -> MEDIA FACTORY (when media is required) -> DISTRIBUTOR -> LABORATORY -> ORACLE -> MAGNIFIER`

Supporting economic path:

`ORACLE/ASSET -> MERCHANT -> VALVE -> QUALIFIED MONEY FLOW`

Persistent control/memory:

`PORTFOLIO allocates oxygen`

`VAULT preserves transferable value`

Mnem/Atlas remain broader OTHRYS knowledge/memory organs; Vault specifically preserves Hyperion-relevant asset/IP/provenance state and must integrate rather than duplicate canonical memory.

## 3. Boundary Laws

Prospector discovers; it does not authorize builds.
Story Forge creates narrative structure; Media Factory manufactures artifacts.
Media Factory produces; Distributor gets artifacts discovered.
Laboratory designs tests; Oracle interprets accumulated evidence.
Oracle detects winners; Magnifier expands them.
Merchant designs commerce; Valve authorizes or blocks financial flow.
Portfolio allocates resources; it does not falsify evidence to defend an allocation.
Vault preserves assets; it does not become a dumping ground for secrets or unnecessary personal data.

## 4. Common Arm Contract

Every mature Arm should expose: mission; inputs; outputs; authority; prohibited actions; dependencies; evidence requirements; telemetry; failure behavior; Course Mode behavior; memory contract; capability Blocks; tests; escalation paths; operator controls.

## 5. Arm Communication

Handoffs use typed artifacts rather than vague prose where practical: Opportunity Card, Story Seed, Fact Pack, Experiment Contract, Media Production Contract, Distribution Plan, Oracle Verdict, Scale Packet, Commercial Readiness Packet, Gate Decision, Allocation Decision and Asset/Provenance Record.

## 6. Course Mode

The Arms may discover, build, test, publish permitted free experiments, measure, preserve, model economics and prepare commercial machinery. Valve enforces the global financial lock. No other Arm may route around it.

## 7. Children's Media Example

`Prospector: children ask why ordinary things work`
`Story Forge: turns manhole-cover fact into mystery/adventure`
`Media Factory: produces native Short/episode`
`Distributor: publishes/routes it`
`Laboratory: tests bounded hook/format hypothesis`
`Oracle: measures retention/return/share/comprehension signals`
`Magnifier: expands proven characters/topics/formats`
`Vault: preserves IP, story bible, assets and provenance`
`Merchant: models future book/app/licensing options`
`Valve: remains SEALED during Course Mode`
`Portfolio: decides how much oxygen the property receives`

## 8. Anti-Blob Law

No Arm should become a synonym for Hyperion itself. If an Arm starts owning discovery + creation + distribution + interpretation + money, boundaries have collapsed. Split responsibilities back to the canonical handoffs.

## 9. Final Laws

> **ONE TITAN. MANY ARMS. ONE VALUE LOOP.**

> **SPECIALIZE AUTHORITY. STANDARDIZE HANDOFFS. PRESERVE EVIDENCE.**

> **NO ARM MAY SILENTLY INHERIT ANOTHER ARM'S AUTHORITY.**

> **THE ARMS EXIST TO MAKE HYPERION MORE INTELLIGENT, NOT MORE COMPLICATED.**
