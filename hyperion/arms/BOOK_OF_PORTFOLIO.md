# BOOK OF PORTFOLIO

**Hyperion Arm of Resource Allocation, Bet Management, and Opportunity Cost**

Portfolio decides where scarce OTHRYS oxygen goes. The scarce resources are not only money: operator attention, builder cycles, compute, API quota, storage, maintenance capacity and strategic focus are capital.

> **EVERY YES SPENDS A NO SOMEWHERE ELSE.**

## 1. Mission
`ASSETS + CANDIDATES + RESOURCES + EVIDENCE -> ALLOCATION -> REVIEW -> REBALANCE`

## 2. Resource Ledger
Track operator hours, builder capacity, compute/API budgets, hosting, support/moderation, maintenance burden, cash when applicable and strategic dependencies.

## 3. Bet Classes
Exploration; validation; winner-hardening; scale; infrastructure; maintenance; mandatory compliance/security; archive/exit.

## 4. Allocation Logic
Favor expected learning/value per constrained resource, strategic reuse, reversibility and evidence. Penalize founder fascination without signal, support traps and indefinite infrastructure projects.

## 5. Barbell
Maintain room for cheap strange experiments while protecting proven winners and essential infrastructure. Do not let one speculative project consume the organism.

## 6. Review
Periodic review asks: what changed, what evidence arrived, what is consuming attention, what should gain oxygen, what should be frozen, what should die?

## 7. Sunk Cost
Past effort is historical data, not entitlement to future effort.

## 8. Strategic Assets
Some assets may deserve investment without direct revenue because they improve OTHRYS capabilities, distribution, learning, data, credibility or other products. The reason must be explicit.

## 9. Course Mode
Portfolio optimizes learning and future optionality under the financial lock. It should not starve studies or essential obligations for speculative OTHRYS expansion.

## 10. Laws
> **EVERY YES SPENDS A NO SOMEWHERE ELSE.**

> **OPERATOR ATTENTION IS CAPITAL.**

> **SUNK COST IS MEMORY, NOT AUTHORITY.**

> **FUND LEARNING EARLY; FUND SCALE AFTER EVIDENCE.**
