# BOOK OF VALVE

**Hyperion Arm of Financial Gates, Revenue Airlocks, and Fail-Closed Economic Authority**

Valve separates popularity and commercial readiness from actual money flow.

> **VIRALITY MAY OPEN AN OPPORTUNITY. IT MUST NEVER OPEN THE CASH REGISTER.**

## 1. Mission
`COMMERCIAL_READY -> AUTHORITY -> GLOBAL GATE -> PRODUCT GATE -> CHANNEL GATE -> RUNTIME GUARDS -> TRANSACTION`

## 2. Hierarchy
Global: `LOCKED | UNLOCKED`
Product: `CLOSED | ELIGIBLE | OPEN | SUSPENDED`
Channel: `DISABLED | PREPARING | READY | ENABLED | SUSPENDED`

Money flows only when every required layer permits it.

## 3. Course Mode Revenue Airlock
```yaml
media_revenue_airlock:
  default: SEALED
  platform_ads: OFF
  affiliates: OFF
  sponsorships: OFF
  memberships: OFF
  tips_donations: OFF
  paid_products: OFF
  licensing_acceptance: HOLD
  audience_growth: ALLOWED
  analytics: ALLOWED
  monetization_eligibility: RECORD_ONLY
  commercial_offer_detected: ESCALATE
```
Exact legal treatment of unexpected income is not guessed; uncertain cases become `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## 4. Authority
Operator/root governance controls opening unless later explicit policy delegates bounded authority. Defined failures may close automatically.

> **OPENING REQUIRES AUTHORITY. CLOSING MAY BE AUTOMATIC.**

## 5. Unexpected Revenue
If a platform, customer or third party creates an unexpected money event, do not assume ignoring, holding or quarantining it changes its legal/tax/benefit status. Record event, prevent further flow where possible, preserve evidence and escalate for appropriate review.

## 6. Runtime Guards
Legal/admin status, account state, product quality, fraud/abuse, capacity, pricing, entitlement, payment health and other declared guards may block or suspend channels.

## 7. Audit
Every gate transition records who/what changed it, previous/new state, evidence, reason, time and affected channels.

## 8. Fail Closed
Unknown authority, stale status, contradictory configuration, missing review or broken guard cannot become permission.
