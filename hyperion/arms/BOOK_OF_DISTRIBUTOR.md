# BOOK OF DISTRIBUTOR

**Hyperion Arm of Distribution Strategy, Discovery Economics, Routing, and Owned-Audience Growth**

Distributor solves the problem after creation: how the right people legitimately encounter the thing.

> **IF VALUE CANNOT BE FOUND, VALUE CANNOT COMPOUND.**

## 1. Mission
`ASSET/PROPERTY -> AUDIENCE -> SURFACE HYPOTHESIS -> ROUTE -> DISCOVERY -> RESPONSE -> ECONOMIC LEARNING`

## 2. Channels
Search/SEO; YouTube discovery; Shorts/Reels/TikTok; marketplaces; app/plugin stores; GitHub/package registries; directories; communities where participation is legitimate; newsletters; partnerships; shareable artifacts; owned web; internal cross-routing.

## 3. Audience-Surface Fit
Distributor never demands omnipresence. Each property gets channels supported by audience evidence and production economics.

## 4. Discovery Architecture
Distribution is designed into products: useful public pages, shareable reports, searchable answers, embeds, exports, scores, references, legitimate backlinks, marketplace fit and obvious next steps.

## 5. Owned Surface Doctrine
Platforms are rented. Where appropriate, preserve a direct lawful relationship through owned site, feed, newsletter, account or downloadable asset without coercive funnels.

## 6. Routing Strategy
Routes should be useful: Short->long explainer; historical video->map; free diagnostic->tool; episode->printable; benchmark post->live benchmark. Do not funnel people simply because a funnel exists.

Distributor chooses/tests **which route is strategically valuable**. For media, Theia owns the actual native package and authorized publication workflow.

## 7. Packaging Boundary

Distributor may specify a distribution hypothesis such as:
- "test YouTube Search";
- "test Eos -> Helios routing";
- "test owned-site capture";
- "test two title promises."

For Theia media, Distributor does **not** own:
- thumbnail rendering;
- title/description artifact production;
- media metadata packaging;
- upload/scheduling execution;
- media platform API side effects.

Those are Theia media-production responsibilities under the declared experiment and authority.

For non-media products, the relevant product/system owns its own packaging/execution.

## 8. Telemetry and Economics
Track and interpret distribution-facing evidence such as impressions, CTR where meaningful, search position where available, qualified visits, referrals, audience/source mix, owned migration, acquisition cost and downstream value events.

Theia may collect media telemetry. Distributor uses returned evidence to evaluate discovery/channel fit and route economics.

## 9. Anti-Spam
No automated unsolicited messaging, comment spam, fake engagement, keyword stuffing, deceptive thumbnails, irrelevant community posting or platform-term evasion.

## 10. Laws
> **IF VALUE CANNOT BE FOUND, VALUE CANNOT COMPOUND.**

> **BE WHERE THE AUDIENCE IS. DO NOT PERFORM OMNIPRESENCE.**

> **CHOOSE THE DISCOVERY SURFACE. LET THE OWNER OF THE ARTIFACT PACKAGE IT NATIVELY.**

> **RENT DISTRIBUTION. OWN THE ASSET.**
