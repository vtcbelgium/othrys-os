# BOOK OF FISHING FLEET

**Hyperion Arm of Rapid Product Experiments and Tiny Useful Products**

Fishing Fleet turns qualified opportunities into the cheapest credible things reality can judge.

> **MANY LINES. SMALL BAIT. REAL WATER.**

## 1. Mission
`CANDIDATE -> MINIMUM PROOF -> BUILD -> VERIFY -> EXPOSE -> MEASURE -> KEEP / ITERATE / KILL / EXPAND`

## 2. Candidate Classes
Calculators, checkers, converters, diagnostics/Doctors, generators, directories, monitors, benchmarks, micro-SaaS, pay-per-run agents, plugins, extensions, workflow packs, public data utilities, tiny educational tools and landing-page demand probes where truthful.

## 3. Minimum Credible Product
Small does not mean broken. Every experiment declares core value event, target user, critical path, evidence gate, telemetry, cost ceiling, time box and kill condition.

## 4. Build Doctrine
Reuse admitted OTHRYS Blocks before inventing bespoke infrastructure. Prefer reversible architecture and disposable shells around reusable capabilities. Avoid premature account systems, billing, dashboards and enterprise abstractions.

## 5. Verification
No experiment goes public merely because generation finished. Critical path, failure behavior, privacy/security where relevant, data integrity, mobile/accessibility where relevant, deployment and rollback must meet the declared contract.

## 6. Experiment Economics
Track time-to-public, compute/API cost, operator attention, support burden, failure rate and marginal cost. A cheap experiment teaches quickly; a hidden maintenance trap is not cheap.

## 7. Fleet Management
Many boats may exist, but inactive experiments must not create operational rot. Every vessel has owner/status, last evidence date, dependencies and archive/kill path.

## 8. Course Mode
All financial adapters default disabled. Fleet products may become popular and gather legitimate usage evidence. Popularity never opens revenue.

## 9. Graduation
A product graduates when evidence justifies hardening. Graduation means a new contract: reliability, ownership, documentation, security, support, economics and distribution expectations rise.

## 10. Anti-Slop
No useless SEO pages, fake tools, deceptive demand tests, duplicated wrappers, unverified outputs or products that secretly require Jeroen to manually rescue every user.

## 11. Laws
> **MANY LINES. SMALL BAIT. REAL WATER.**

> **THE EXPERIMENT IS BUILT TO LEARN, NOT TO IMPRESS ITS BUILDER.**

> **SMALL MAY BE TEMPORARY. BROKEN MAY NOT.**

> **KILL CHEAPLY. GRADUATE DELIBERATELY.**
