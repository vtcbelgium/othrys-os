# BOOK OF STORY FORGE

**Theia Arm of Story Intelligence, Narrative Generation, and Original IP Formation**

Story Forge converts facts, questions, concepts and strategic messages into stories worth following. It is not a script prompt. It is a memory-bearing creative system that learns narrative mechanics while preserving originality, factual truth and continuity.

> **THE FACT IS THE TREASURE. THE STORY IS THE HUNT.**

## 1. Mission

`SEED -> FACT PACK -> STORY QUESTION -> GENOME -> WORLD/CAST -> MYSTERY -> CLUES -> COMPLICATION -> DISCOVERY -> PAYOFF -> CONTINUITY -> SCRIPT CONTRACT`

Story Forge may serve children's education, explainers, documentaries, product narratives, games, books, interactive stories and other admitted media/IP.

## 2. Four Chambers

**Story Mine** collects usable seeds: facts, mysteries, places, objects, historical events, public-domain folklore, questions, contrasts, human problems and original operator ideas.

**Story Forge** recombines seeds with abstract Story Genes and format constraints to create original narrative candidates.

**Story Bible** owns continuity: characters, motives, relationships, world rules, chronology, recurring objects, unresolved clues, tone, vocabulary, age band, visual constraints and forbidden contradictions.

**Story Laboratory** generates bounded variants and learns which mechanics actually produce attention, comprehension, emotion and return behavior.

## 3. Story Genes

Reusable abstract genes include mysterious object; treasure hunt; map fragment; hidden room; message from past; apparent ghost; mistaken legend; historical visitor; time displacement; miniature journey; broken machine; race against time; rival seeker; secret symbol; impossible object; lost invention; rescue mission; archaeological discovery; folklore investigation; transformation; journey to source; false culprit; three clues; escalating attempts; return-with-proof.

Genes are mechanics, not copied expression.

## 4. Rights Firewall

Story Forge may learn high-level mechanics from successful works. It must not reproduce protected characters, dialogue, distinctive plots, art, fictional devices, branding or confusing combinations. Inspiration provenance is retained. Similarity/collision checks become stronger as an IP approaches publication or commercial value.

> **HARVEST THE ENGINE. DO NOT STEAL THE CAR.**

## 5. Educational Story Contract

For factual entertainment, label internally: `FACT`, `SIMPLIFICATION`, `ANALOGY`, `SPECULATION`, `FICTION`.

The factual payload is independently researched before story generation. Fiction may carry the viewer to the fact but may not quietly rewrite it.

## 6. Story Quality Test

A story candidate should have a question the viewer wants answered, an immediate disturbance, agency, progression, at least one meaningful turn, a payoff, emotional texture and a reason for each scene.

If removing the educational fact leaves no story worth watching, strengthen the story. If removing the story leaves an ordinary classroom explanation, strengthen the adventure.

## 7. Story Genome

Canonical children's baseline:

`ODD THING -> QUESTION -> CLUE -> JOURNEY -> WRONG GUESS -> DISCOVERY -> COMPLICATION -> TRUE EXPLANATION -> SMALL PAYOFF -> NEXT CLUE`

This is a starting genome, not a mandatory template. Story Laboratory should evolve multiple genomes to avoid formula fatigue.

## 8. Character Intelligence

Characters need stable wants, fears, competencies, blind spots, humor roles, relationships and growth boundaries. Characters should generate story naturally. A curious impulsive explorer and a careful verifier produce useful tension because their disagreement creates investigation.

Do not let every character speak with generic AI competence.

## 9. Long Arc

Individual episodes resolve their main promise while optionally advancing a larger quest. Long arcs may use recovered pages, symbols, maps, rival objectives, world mysteries or character goals. Continuity hooks must reward returning viewers without making standalone episodes incomprehensible.

## 10. Variant Generation

For a seed, Story Forge may produce alternative hooks, settings, stakes, genes, cast assignments, reveals and endings. Story Laboratory changes bounded variables rather than randomly rewriting everything.

## 11. Feedback Learning

Store retention drops, rewatches, comments/questions, comprehension problems, favorite characters, successful reveals, recurring requests and production difficulty. Never infer a universal law from one viral episode.

## 12. Anti-Slop

Reject exposition wearing a costume; arbitrary peril; fake cliffhangers; interchangeable characters; lore without payoff; repeated plots with changed nouns; fact dumping; random jokes; unexplained magic in factual segments; copied creator voice; and stories whose only appeal is synthetic visual novelty.

## 13. Outputs

Story Seed Card; Fact/fiction boundary map; Story Genome; beat sheet; continuity delta; script contract; visual opportunities; next-episode hooks; provenance record; risk flags.

Story Forge does not itself publish.

## 14. Course Mode

Original worlds, characters, story banks, scripts and pilots may be developed and tested while commercial gates remain closed. A breakout IP is preserved, not automatically monetized.

## 15. Laws

> **THE FACT IS THE TREASURE. THE STORY IS THE HUNT.**

> **CURIOSITY CREATES THE QUEST; THE QUEST DELIVERS THE FACT.**

> **CONTINUITY IS MEMORY MADE ENTERTAINING.**

> **FORMULA SHOULD CREATE RELIABILITY, NOT PREDICTABILITY.**

> **A STORY GENERATOR THAT DOES NOT LEARN BECOMES A SLOP GENERATOR.**

## 16. Property-Grade Script Room

The generic Story Forge remains the narrative authority for media properties. A recurring animated property may add a **property profile** rather than invent a second story engine.

The reference profile is `theia/properties/pemberton/BOOK_OF_PEMBERTON.md`.

For property-grade episodic work, use a staged script room:

`CANON LOAD -> PREMISE -> STORY ENGINE -> CHARACTER PASS -> EMBODIMENT PASS -> CHILD-CLARITY PASS -> ADULT-LAYER PASS -> COMEDY PASS -> ARC PASS -> RIGHTS PASS -> DIALOGUE REDUCTION -> TABLE READ -> HUMAN LOCK`

The passes are separable so a weak script can be repaired at the layer that failed instead of randomly regenerated.

### 16.1 Canon / Working / Fossil

A recurring property benefits from three explicit creative-memory states:

- **CANON** — approved truths that may not silently drift;
- **WORKING** — mutable development material;
- **FOSSIL** — rejected ideas retained with rejection reasons.

Fossil memory prevents models and humans from repeatedly rediscovering ideas already rejected for character, rights, tone or continuity reasons.

This does not replace Mnemosyne. Story Forge maintains property-local creative state; durable institutional knowledge remains subject to normal OTHRYS memory governance.

### 16.2 Character Embodiment Test

A character should not merely sound distinctive. The **physical solution space** should also be character-specific.

For every episode ask:

- Could a generic protagonist perform exactly the same actions?
- Does the character's body, habits, limitations, preferences or blind spots materially shape the plot?
- Does the visual behavior carry information that dialogue need not repeat?

For Pemberton specifically, a feline-action pass rejects human-like problem solving when a believable cat action can carry the beat.

### 16.3 Gag Ledger

Recurring comedy is continuity and should be tracked deliberately.

At minimum record:
- gag ID;
- first use;
- last use;
- variant used;
- escalation state;
- cooldown;
- intended future payoff.

A running joke should evolve rather than repeat identical wording.

### 16.4 Arc Knowledge Split

For serialized mystery, track separately:
- what the audience knows;
- what each protagonist knows;
- what the antagonist knows;
- what supporting characters know;
- which promises remain unpaid.

This prevents accidental premature reveals and fake mystery created by characters forgetting facts they already learned.
