# BOOK OF THEIA — EXPLAINER & INSTRUCTIONAL ARM

**Purpose:** transform verified knowledge or practical procedures into original visual media that makes something easier to understand or do.

> **MAKE THE INVISIBLE LEGIBLE. MAKE THE DIFFICULT DOABLE.**

## 1. Core classes

- practical how-to;
- educational explainer;
- science/history/geography;
- product walkthrough;
- process explainer;
- whiteboard/doodle;
- cartoon character explainer;
- motion-graphics/data explainer;
- animated diagram.

## 2. Production methods

### Template/rigged 2D
Strong for early proof:
- consistent characters;
- reusable scenes;
- predictable cost;
- easy revision.

### Programmatic vector/video
Strong long-term:
- deterministic;
- data-driven;
- versionable;
- batchable;
- portable;
- excellent for diagrams, text, charts and repeated visual grammar.

### Whiteboard/doodle
Extremely automation-friendly for concepts:
- voice-aligned reveals;
- arrows;
- labels;
- progressive diagrams.

### Generative image/video assistance
Use for:
- special scenes;
- backgrounds;
- visual metaphors;
- difficult one-offs.

Do not assume full generative video is best for:
- exact diagrams;
- recurring-character consistency;
- factual mechanism animation;
- low-cost revisions.

    VERIFIED INPUT

      -> PACKAGE
      -> PUBLISH
      -> LEARN

## 4. Instructional safety

For DIY/practical content:
- distinguish safe beginner tasks from licensed/high-risk work;
- include protective-equipment and shutoff steps where material;
- do not create unsafe confidence;
- cite manufacturer/official procedures when model-specific;
- avoid pretending one method fits every jurisdiction/product;
- escalate electrical/gas/structural/high-risk procedures to professional guidance when appropriate.

## 5. Visual kit strategy

Long-term properties should prefer reusable owned assets:
- character rigs;
- expressions/poses;
- rooms;
- tools;
- props;
- arrows;
- diagrams;
- charts;
- maps;
- transitions;
- title systems;
- caption styles.

Generate missing assets deliberately, review them, then admit reusable ones to the property library.

## 6. Voice

Voice must be:
- licensed for intended use;
- stable enough for continuity;
- pronunciation-controlled;
- replaceable by adapter.

Word/character timing should be preserved when available for captions and animation.

## 7. Monetization paths

Hyperion evaluates commercial opening, but useful Theia products include:

### Productized B2B explainer
Input:
- document/URL;
- audience;
- goal;
- brand kit.

Output:
- branded 30–120 second video;
- captions;
- platform variants.

Low-touch potential is high if revisions are bounded.

### Owned educational channels
Longer path, stronger asset creation.

### Product distribution
Explainers drive adoption of OTHRYS/Oros products.

### White-label engine/API
Later graduation path if Opsis becomes stable.

Platform ad revenue is a channel, not the foundation.

## 8. Anti-template law

Recurring design is good.
Interchangeable intellectual content is bad.

Repeat:
- brand;
- characters;
- visual grammar;
- environment;
- format skeleton.

Vary meaningfully:
- problem;
- facts;
- examples;
- scenes;
- explanation;
- narrative;
- outcome.

## 9. Current adapter research

Early proof:
- Vyond-class cartoon automation;
- similar 2D SaaS;
- modern TTS.

Long-term:
- Remotion-class programmatic video;
- SVG/vector assets;
- deterministic scene components;
- replaceable generation providers.

## 10. Final laws

> **THE VISUAL SHOULD EXPLAIN, NOT MERELY MOVE.**

> **AUTOMATE REPETITION; CURATE MEANING.**

> **A REUSABLE CHARACTER IS AN ASSET. A REPEATED SCRIPT WITH CHANGED NOUNS IS SLOP.**
