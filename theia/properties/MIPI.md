# MIPI — THEIA STARTER PROPERTY BIBLE

**Status:** ORIGINAL IP STARTER — concept locked at seed level, visual identity and world details still experimental.

## 1. Canonical seed

Name: **Mipi**

Species: cat

Pronouns: he/him

Core contradiction:
- intensely curious;
- intensely lazy.

Core joke:
- curiosity gets Mipi into adventures;
- laziness makes him seek the shortest path, the softest surface and the next nap;
- he may fall asleep during an adventure, including at absurd or inconvenient moments.

> **MIPI MUST KNOW WHAT IS AROUND THE CORNER. MIPI ALSO VERY MUCH NEEDS A NAP.**

This contradiction is not just a gag. It creates plot:
- curiosity creates motion;
- laziness creates resistance;
- naps create timing problems, accidental discoveries, dream transitions or comic reversals;
- curiosity wakes him back up.

The nap is a recurring behavior, not a mandatory beat in every episode.

## 2. Promise

A funny, warm, original adventure cat explores the world and accidentally learns real things.

Audience hypothesis:
- family-safe;
- initial test band roughly primary-school age plus co-viewing adults;
- exact age targeting remains experimental.

Value:
- entertainment first enough to earn attention;
- learning embedded in the quest;
- recurring character attachment;
- international/localizable subject matter.

## 3. Originality firewall

The property may harvest broad adventure mechanics from European comics, children's serials, travel stories and mystery structures.

It must not copy:
- Suske en Wiske characters;
- album plots;
- visual designs;
- inventions;
- dialogue;
- branded places;
- distinctive relationships;
- titles.

The useful inheritance is:
- curiosity -> movement;
- real places/history as adventure terrain;
- clue/reveal structures;
- humor beside danger/confusion;
- recurring cast/world continuity.

> **HARVEST THE ADVENTURE GRAMMAR. MIPI MUST BECOME HIS OWN CAT.**

## 4. Personality

Locked:
- curious;
- lazy;
- warm comic energy;
- adventurous despite himself.

Candidate traits to test, not yet locked:
- unexpectedly observant after waking;
- treats any horizontal surface as a bed;
- snack-motivated;
- overconfident about shortcuts;
- hates unnecessary effort;
- loves boxes/bags/maps for cat-like reasons;
- occasionally solves a problem because he was too lazy to follow the obvious route.

Avoid:
- generic hyperactive mascot behavior;
- constant sarcasm;
- making laziness equal stupidity;
- making every problem resolve by accident.

Mipi should sometimes be clever precisely because he conserves effort.

## 5. Story engine

Baseline:

    STRANGE THING
      -> MIPI CANNOT RESIST
      -> INVESTIGATES
      -> TAKES A SHORTCUT / GETS DISTRACTED
      -> CLUE
      -> NAP OR OTHER COMIC RESISTANCE WHEN USEFUL
      -> DISCOVERY
      -> REAL EXPLANATION
      -> SMALL EMOTIONAL/COMIC PAYOFF
      -> NEXT POSSIBILITY

## 6. Educational lanes

Strong:
- everyday-object mysteries;
- science;
- animal/nature;
- geography;
- historical places;
- archaeology;
- folklore vs evidence;
- engineering;
- museums;

Sensitive lanes require stronger review:

- dangerous DIY;

## 7. Mipi + Theia children

**Helios**

**Eos**
- 15–60 second discovery short;
- funniest reveal;
- one compact fact;
- teaser leading to fuller episode where useful.

**Selene**
- continuity;
- playlists;
- gentle recurring story world;
- return behavior;
- possible calmer/bedtime variants later.

## 8. The nap mechanic

Potential uses:
- comic interruption;
- time passing;
- accidental transport;
- dream visualization of invisible mechanisms;
- waking at the exact moment something changes;
- another character having to continue;
- missing the obvious clue and later spotting a subtle one;
- cozy emotional close.

Rule:
Do not repeat the exact "Mipi falls asleep, wakes, solves it" structure mechanically. Platform policies and audience satisfaction both punish interchangeable episodes.

## 9. Visual identity

Not locked yet.

Test families:
- clean European cartoon line;
- soft flat 2D;
- vector rig suitable for automation;
- expressive face/tail/ears;
- readable silhouette at Shorts size.

Long-term preference:
- own character rig;
- own expression/pose library;
- reusable environments;
- programmatic/vector-friendly assets.

Do not lock appearance before pilot tests.

## 10. Voice

Test:
- narrator-led with Mipi reactions;
- Mipi speaking;
- hybrid.

Localization should remain possible.

Avoid a celebrity-imitation voice.

## 12. Long arc

No mandatory magical artifact is canon yet.

Candidates:
- Mipi's travel notebook;
- a growing map;
- recurring places/friends;
- collectible curiosity cards;
- a larger mystery.

Previous "Lost Atlas" concepts remain optional research, not canon.

## 13. Business optionality

Do not force monetization before audience signal.

Potential later surfaces:
- platform revenue;
- books;
- picture books/comics;
- printables;
- educational packs;
- museum/institution collaborations;
- games/apps;
- licensing;
- merchandise only after real affinity;

Hyperion evaluates which deserve opening.

## 14. Safety

Because Mipi is likely child/family-facing:
- no manipulative urgency;
- no disguised ads;
- no unsafe challenges;
- no unnecessary personal-data collection;
- no repetitive fake peril;
- no frightening thumbnails divorced from content;
- sponsorship clearly handled.

## 15. Pilot plan

Batch 1:
- 5–10 shorts with materially different topics;
- 2–3 fuller episodes;
- one or two visual styles, not ten;
- measure production burden and audience retention.

Questions:
- Does Mipi himself improve retention over a generic narrator?
- Does the lazy/curious contradiction create affection?
- Does the nap gag work without becoming predictable?
- Which educational lane fits best?
- Can the animation be produced cheaply enough?

## 16. Mipi laws

> **CURIOSITY STARTS THE ADVENTURE. LAZINESS MAKES IT MIPI.**

> **THE NAP IS CHARACTER, NOT A TEMPLATE.**

> **MIPI MAY BE LAZY. THE WRITING MAY NOT BE.**

> **MAKE CHILDREN WANT THE NEXT ADVENTURE, NOT THE NEXT DOPAMINE HIT.**
