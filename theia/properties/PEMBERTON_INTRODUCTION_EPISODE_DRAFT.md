# PEMBERTON — INTRODUCTION EPISODE DRAFT

**Working title:** *Welcome to Pemberton House*  
**Status:** DRAFT — not canonical story text.  
**Purpose:** introduce Pemberton, his worldview, his household systems, his comedy states, his owners, and the Grand Tour Door in one self-contained opening episode.

**Target shape:** ~5–7 minutes for a fuller introduction episode, with a shorter cut possible later.

---

## 0. CORE IDEA

Pemberton believes he is giving the audience a dignified guided tour of his estate.

In reality, the tour repeatedly exposes:
- his laziness;
- his cat instincts;
- his affection for his owners;
- his household delusions;
- his dependence on automation;
- his cola ritual;
- his fear of the Infernal Atomizer;
- his Play Mode;
- his Pet Mode;
- his tiny wardrobe failures.

The episode ends by revealing that one part of his grandiose worldview is **actually true**:

**Pemberton House contains the Grand Tour Door, and it really can take him on adventures.**

That is the series promise.

> **THE INTRODUCTION SHOULD BEGIN AS A JOKE ABOUT A PAMPERED CAT AND END AS A PROMISE OF A REAL ADVENTURE WORLD.**

---

# 1. COLD OPEN — THE MOTOR CARRIAGE

### INT. MODERN LIVING ROOM — DAY

Low camera.

We see:
- sofa legs;
- coffee table;
- modern charging cables;
- one human pair of legs crossing frame;
- ordinary household clutter.

A soft robotic hum approaches.

From behind the sofa rolls a **robot vacuum**.

Sitting on it:

**PEMBERTON.**

Older tuxedo cat.
Soft bow collar.
Slightly crooked.
Pronounced primordial pouch.
Completely serious.

A generic modern takeaway cola cup sits in a tiny cup holder.

Pemberton rides into frame like a head of state arriving by carriage.

He looks directly toward camera.

**PEMBERTON**
You are early.

Beat.

The robot vacuum continues moving.

Pemberton waits for it to stop.

It does not.

**PEMBERTON**
Carriage.

Still moving.

**PEMBERTON**
...halt.

The vacuum rotates ninety degrees, slowly turning Pemberton away from camera.

Beat.

Pemberton rotates his head back toward us while the rest of him continues turning.

**PEMBERTON**
A minor disagreement concerning route protocol.

The vacuum heads toward a chair.

Pemberton hops off before it goes underneath.

His primordial pouch gives a small delayed sway as he lands.

He straightens the bow.

Tiny dignity reset.

**PEMBERTON**
Welcome to Pemberton House.

TITLE.

---

# 2. THE ESTATE

Pemberton begins walking.

His measured walk gives the audience the pouch sway.

A human shoe is partly in his path.

Pemberton stops.

Looks at shoe.

Looks up toward unseen human.

Looks back at shoe.

He produces a tiny folded napkin/cloth, places it over a questionable patch beside the shoe, steps across, then picks up the cloth by one clean corner.

**PEMBERTON**
The staff are loyal.

Beat.

He looks at the shoe again.

**PEMBERTON**
Hygiene remains developmental.

He walks on.

One crisp hind-paw dismissal flick.

No comment.

---

# 3. SUCCESSFUL STAFF TRAINING

Pemberton reaches a closed interior door.

He sits.

Looks at door.

Looks toward nearby human legs.

One precise meow.

Nothing.

Pemberton waits.

Second meow, slightly different.

Human hand enters frame and opens the door.

Pemberton looks at camera with quiet satisfaction.

**PEMBERTON**
That one took months.

He walks through.

The human begins closing the door.

Pemberton stops halfway.

Human immediately reopens it.

Pemberton continues without looking back.

**PEMBERTON**
One must be consistent.

This establishes:
- owners as staff;
- Pemberton's command pride;
- human affection;
- his delusion without cruelty.

---

# 4. THE LOYAL STEWARD

A timed automatic feeder activates.

**CLICK — WHIRR — FOOD DROPS.**

Pemberton stops mid-tour.

Looks at feeder.

Looks at camera.

He gives a tiny approving nod.

**PEMBERTON**
The Loyal Steward.

He walks over and checks the bowl.

**PEMBERTON**
Punctual.
Discreet.
Remarkably resistant to conversation.

Human legs pass behind him carrying laundry.

Pemberton glances after them.

**PEMBERTON**
The others could learn from him.

He does not eat yet.

This is a tour.
Standards must be maintained.

He reluctantly continues.

# 5. THE SANITATION BUREAU

Pemberton exits a modern self-cleaning litter box area.

Do not show the actual act.

He walks away with complete composure.

A beat later:

**WHIRR.**

The litter machine begins its cleaning cycle behind him.

Pemberton does not turn around.

**PEMBERTON**
Sanitation has been notified.

He keeps walking.

This should be almost throwaway.

# 6. PEMBERTON HOUSE

The modern room opens onto an elaborate cat house / cat condo.

From human scale it is clearly extravagant pet furniture.

From Pemberton's camera height it becomes:

**PEMBERTON HOUSE.**

Visual details:
- dark wood;
- brass;
- tiny map;
- miniature globe;
- postcards;
- stamps;
- tickets;
- coins;
- tiny shelf;
- blanket;
- satchel;
- travel labels;
- little clock;
- porcelain teacup and saucer.

Pemberton steps onto a small platform.

**PEMBERTON**
Pemberton House.

Beat.

**PEMBERTON**
Library.
Archive.
Expedition office.
Refreshment room.
Private quarters.

Camera reveals this is all packed into a glorified cat condo.

Pemberton notices audience reaction.

**PEMBERTON**
Efficient use of space is a mark of civilization.

# 7. COLA SERVICE

He moves to a tiny desk.

There is a porcelain teacup.

Pemberton pours or receives fictional cat-safe cola from a tiny unbranded vessel.

Fizz.

He inspects the bubbles.

Takes a small sip.

Tiny feline "pinky" gesture.

**PEMBERTON**
Properly chilled.
Adequate carbonation.
Ice restrained.

He looks at camera.

**PEMBERTON**
One need not be wealthy to have standards.

From elsewhere, the modern takeaway cup is visible.

**PEMBERTON**
Porcelain at home.
Field vessel on expedition.

He points toward the takeaway cup.

**PEMBERTON**
Ugly.

Beat.

**PEMBERTON**
Excellent lid.

# 8. PLAY MODE INTERRUPTION

Pemberton stands in front of the map wall.

**PEMBERTON**
The educated traveller must maintain concentration at all—

A feather wand enters frame behind him, moved by a human hand.

Pemberton stops.

Tiny micro-freeze.

Pupils BLOOM.

**PRRT!**

Pizzicato snap.

His head tracks the feather.

His body lowers.

Rear rises.

Pronounced little hindquarter wiggle.

Pouch follows with secondary wobble.

He is still trying to talk.

**PEMBERTON**
—at... all...

Feather twitches.

Pemberton LAUNCHES.

Out of frame.

Small crash / rustle.

Silence.

Pemberton walks back in.

Feather stuck to one side of his bow.

He freezes when he realizes we are watching.

Removes feather.

Straightens bow.

Tiny throat clear.

**PEMBERTON**
Silly me.

Beat.

**PEMBERTON**
As I was saying: concentration.

He continues.

---

# 9. COOTIES -> PET MODE

A human hand enters frame to remove a remaining feather from his shoulder.

Pemberton recoils.

Produces tiny cloth barrier.

**PEMBERTON**
Please.

The human hand instead finds the underside of his chin.

Scratch.

Pemberton freezes.

Eyes soften.

Head rises.

Whiskers sweep forward.

**MRRP...**

Warm Purr Bloom.

Pemberton leans into the scratch despite himself.

His bow is gradually pushed sideways.

He is completely gone.

Human hand stops.

Pemberton remains in position for one beat too long.

Eyes open.

He realizes.

Slowly lowers head.

Straightens bow.

**PEMBERTON**
Your technique is acceptable.

He gives the hand a suspicious look.

**PEMBERTON**
Your hygiene remains questionable.

Then, quieter:

**PEMBERTON**
Continue.

Human hand returns for one tiny scratch.

Pemberton immediately melts again.

Cut.

---

# 10. THE INFERNAL ATOMIZER

Pemberton resumes the tour beside a shelf.

On the shelf is a small object near the edge.

Pemberton notices it.

He gives camera a scholarly look.

**PEMBERTON**
A practical demonstration of gravity.

He raises one paw to push it.

Off-screen human voice:

**OWNER**
NO!

Pemberton's paw freezes one millimetre from the object.

Silence.

His pupils widen.

His eyes move—not his head—toward a nearby counter.

There sits:

**THE SPRAY BOTTLE.**

Ordinary plastic.

But from his angle its molding subtly resembles a skull.

Tiny trigger creak.

No spray yet.

Low bassoon note.

Pemberton slowly withdraws his paw.

**PEMBERTON**
The experiment had concluded.

He steps down.

Walks away.

One sharp hind-paw dismissal flick.

Then, without turning around:

**PEMBERTON**
The staff have adopted an unfortunate policy framework.

Human hand picks up the object and puts it safely farther back.

Pemberton glances at the hand.

No resentment.

Only the bottle receives the death stare.

---

# 11. THE MOTOR CARRIAGE HOUSE

Pemberton returns to the robot vacuum.

It is docked.

**PEMBERTON**
Local transportation.

He gestures at the vacuum.

**PEMBERTON**
The Motor Carriage.

He gestures at the charging dock.

**PEMBERTON**
Carriage House.

Beat.

The vacuum begins an automated cleaning cycle unexpectedly.

Pemberton pauses.

**PEMBERTON**
It has initiative.

The vacuum moves away without him.

Long beat.

**PEMBERTON**
Too much initiative.

He follows at a dignified pace rather than running.

# 12. THE HIDDEN ROOM

The vacuum passes near Pemberton House.

Pemberton steps off / redirects us toward a less obvious section behind the map wall.

His tone changes slightly.

Less pompous.

More genuinely excited.

**PEMBERTON**
Now.

He checks that the humans are not immediately nearby.

**PEMBERTON**
There is one part of the estate the staff do not entirely understand.

This is important.

For the first time, his grandiose claim is not merely comic.

He pushes aside a map / opens a small panel.

Behind it:
- brass;
- tiny dial;
- mechanical selector;
- slots for postcards, coins, tickets or stamps;
- a miniature world map;
- a narrow ornate door.

**THE GRAND TOUR DOOR.**

Music shifts from comedy to wonder.

# 13. THE GRAND TOUR DOOR

Pemberton takes out a postcard.

Maybe:
- pyramids;
- a lighthouse;
- Roman ruins;
- an old railway station;
- a natural landmark.

He inserts or places it into the travel mechanism.

A brass indicator moves.

Map marker shifts.

The tiny mechanism wakes.

**PEMBERTON**
A question may begin anywhere.

He turns a dial.

**PEMBERTON**
A stamp.
A coin.
A postcard.
A ticket.
A map.

The Door hums.

**PEMBERTON**
Give the House a destination—

He locks the selector.

**PEMBERTON**
—and it gives me a way in.

The Door opens.

Beyond it is not a generic glowing portal.

We see a **real place**.

Wind moves through.

A bit of dust blows into Pemberton House.

Pemberton immediately looks offended.

He places a napkin over his cola.

**PEMBERTON**
Naturally, one must prepare.

He closes the Door again.

The room becomes quiet.

Looks toward the Door.

# 19. CORE DIRECTING RULE

The audience should finish the episode understanding:

**Pemberton thinks he is a world-class gentleman explorer running a sophisticated estate.**

The audience should also understand:

**he is a spoiled old cat whose humans love him very much.**

And finally:

**he really does have a door to impossible adventures.**

That third truth is what turns the comedy premise into a series.

> **FIRST WE LAUGH AT HIS WORLDVIEW. THEN THE DOOR OPENS.**
