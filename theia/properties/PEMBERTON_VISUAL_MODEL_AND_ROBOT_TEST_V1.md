# PEMBERTON — VISUAL MODEL + ROomba ANIMATION TEST V1

**Status:** DRAFT PRODUCTION SPEC — not final visual canon.
**Style direction:** Franco-Belgian clear-line, modern animation economy, Belle Époque environmental accents.
**Purpose:** lock enough of Pemberton's visual grammar to test the opening Motor Carriage scene in motion.

---

## 1. MODEL DIRECTION

Pemberton should look funny while standing still.

### Face
- older-cat intelligence without frailty;
- compact feline muzzle;
- slightly heavy upper eyelids in Gentleman Mode;
- clear white muzzle/chin area;
- brows implied through eye shape/fur rather than human eyebrows;
- eyes restrained at rest so Play Mode pupil bloom has room to transform him;
- whiskers long and readable in silhouette;
- ears expressive but not oversized preschool triangles.

### Body
- recognizably quadrupedal cat;
- comfortably built, not spherical;
- pronounced primordial pouch;
- chest broad enough to support dignified silhouette;
- compact legs;
- real-cat seated posture;
- tail thick enough to read clearly in animation;
- explosive agility remains plausible.

### Tuxedo markings
V1 target:
- black crown and ears;
- white central muzzle/chin;
- white bib flowing into chest;
- white front paws;
- restrained white rear-paw markings;
- avoid a perfectly symmetrical "mascot mask";
- markings must remain easy to reproduce frame to frame.

Final marking lock waits for model-sheet comparison.

### Bow collar
- ordinary pet collar;
- small deep-red fabric bow;
- small round brass/gold-toned tag;
- bow must be able to rotate, fold, and be pushed sideways;
- never large enough to read as costume cosplay.

### Pouch
The pouch must be visible in side/three-quarter walk views.
Motion principle:
- body moves first;
- pouch follows 2–4 frames later in limited animation;
- tiny final secondary beat on stop;
- never exaggerated like gelatin.

### Default expression
Pemberton's neutral should read:
- mildly unimpressed;
- observant;
- dignified;
- comfortable;
- not angry.

He should look like he has already judged the room and found it serviceable.

## 2. VISUAL STYLE

### Character rendering
- crisp dark ink line;
- slight hand-drawn variation;
- flat-to-lightly-modelled color;
- minimal fur texture;
- no photoreal fur;
- no 3D-render sheen;
- no generic preschool vector gloss.

### Environment
Modern home:
- recognizably contemporary;
- natural clutter;
- clean readable geometry;
- warmer palette than sterile modern minimalism.

Pemberton House:
- denser detail;
- brass;
- mahogany;
- maps;
- travel ephemera;
- Belle Époque / explorer-club flavor.

The visual joke depends on:
**ordinary modern world + Pemberton's grand interpretation.**

### Camera
- low cat-height perspective;
- humans framed roughly knee-down;
- hands may enter;
- no owner faces.

---

## 3. ROOBA TEST — 24 SECOND OPENING

### SHOT 01 — 0:00–0:03
**Wide low-angle modern living room.**

No Pemberton yet.

Audio:
- quiet room tone;
- faint robot-vacuum motor approaching from off-screen;
- no music for first second.

Goal:
let the audience hear him coming before seeing him.

### SHOT 02 — 0:03–0:07
Motor Carriage glides into frame.

Pemberton sits upright on top.
Takeaway cola in tiny cup holder.
Bow is approximately 5 degrees crooked.
Pouch compressed naturally in seated pose.

Human legs cross in deep background without acknowledging him.

Pemberton notices camera/viewer.

Small eye movement first.
Then head.

**PEMBERTON**
"You are early."

Performance:
completely serious.
No wink.
No smile.

Music:
tiny refined chamber motif enters after line.

### SHOT 03 — 0:07–0:11
The Motor Carriage keeps moving past the intended stopping point.

Pemberton remains still for one beat.

**PEMBERTON**
"Carriage."

Nothing.

One eye shifts downward.

**PEMBERTON**
"...halt."

No panic.

### SHOT 04 — 0:11–0:15
Robot vacuum begins a slow route-correction rotation.

Pemberton rotates away from viewer with it.

He does not move his body.

Only his head slowly turns back toward us until his neck reaches a slightly ridiculous but believable cat angle.

**PEMBERTON**
"A minor disagreement concerning route protocol."

This is the first major laugh.

No additional gag sound.

Let the image work.

### SHOT 05 — 0:15–0:19
Vacuum approaches chair/low furniture.

Pemberton calculates.

One quick, surprisingly athletic hop off.

Landing:
- front paws;
- rear paws;
- pouch follows with one soft delayed wobble.

He remains perfectly composed.

Motor Carriage continues out of frame.

### SHOT 06 — 0:19–0:22
Pemberton notices bow is crooked.

Tiny freeze.

One paw attempts adjustment.
Fails slightly.
Second tiny correction succeeds.

Small throat clear.

Audio:
- restrained collar-fabric flick;
- one dry pizzicato note.

### SHOT 07 — 0:22–0:24
Pemberton looks directly toward viewer.

Behind him, the Motor Carriage bumps gently into something off-screen.

Tiny *bonk*.

Pemberton does not look back.

**PEMBERTON**
"Welcome to Pemberton House."

CUT TO TITLE.

## 4. VOICE DIRECTION

Pemberton should sound:
- male;
- older but not elderly;
- warm;
- educated;
- mellow;
- slightly resonant;
- precise;
- faintly theatrical through habit, not performance;
- never cartoonishly British;
- never snobbish enough to become cold.

The actor should behave as though:
**everything Pemberton says is reasonable.**

Humor comes from the situation, not comic delivery.

### Pace
- slightly slower than an excitable children's presenter;
- short pauses before dry observations;
- quicker only during genuine curiosity;
- Play Mode may interrupt sentence rhythm entirely.

### Test lines
1. "You are early."
2. "Carriage."
3. "...halt."
4. "A minor disagreement concerning route protocol."
5. "Welcome to Pemberton House."

Voice candidates should be judged on line 4.
If that line is funny without the actor trying to be funny, the voice is close.

---

## 5. SOUND DESIGN

### Motor Carriage
- real soft electric vacuum hum;
- subtle wheel direction change;
- no cartoon engine sound.

### Bow adjustment
- tiny cloth flick;
- optional single dry string pluck.

### Pouch
No sound.

The pouch joke is purely visual.

### Environment
- floor creak;
- distant household movement;
- room tone;
- optional faint feeder click later in full episode.

### Music
Opening cue:
- light chamber ensemble;
- pizzicato strings / clarinet / bassoon possibility;
- adventurous but intimate;
- not "Victorian parody" music;
- not circus music.

---

## 6. ANIMATION ECONOMY

The scene should rely on held poses and small changes.

Important:
- do not overanimate Pemberton;
- stillness = dignity;
- sudden movement = catness.

Approximate motion hierarchy:
1. eyes;
2. ears;
3. head;
4. tail;
5. body;
6. explosive full-body action only when earned.

The comedy dies if he is constantly bouncing.

## 8. PASS / FAIL TEST

The design passes if:
- Pemberton is recognizable without text;
- the Roomba gag reads immediately;
- he looks like a cat, not a small human in cat skin;
- older-cat status reads without frailty;
- primordial pouch reads without obesity caricature;
- neutral eyes leave room for dramatic pupil bloom;
- bow collar reads as pet collar and formalwear simultaneously;
- he can be funny while almost motionless;
- the world feels Franco-Belgian rather than generic AI children's art.

The animation test passes if a viewer understands, within 24 seconds:
1. this cat thinks he is important;
2. the robot vacuum is his transport;
3. the modern world does not cooperate with his dignity;
4. he is physically a real cat;
5. Pemberton House is somewhere worth following him into.

> **STILLNESS IS GENTLEMAN MODE. MOTION IS WHERE THE CAT ESCAPES.**
