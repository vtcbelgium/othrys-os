# THEIA CHILDREN FOUNDATIONS

**Status:** CANONICAL FOUNDATIONS — names and strategic meanings are reserved; none is an independent Titan/runtime authority today.

Mythological family:
- Theia + Hyperion -> Helios, Selene, Eos.

OTHRYS family:
- Theia owns media production.
- Hyperion supplies/receives economic intelligence through the sibling contract.
- The three children name recurring **audience-facing expression roles** only when useful.

They are **composable labels, not mutually exclusive pipelines**.

Examples:
- flagship recurring episode = Helios + Selene;
- recurring discovery short = Eos + Selene;
- one-off definitive explainer = Helios.

Not every artifact needs a child label. A label does not create runtime authority.

## HELIOS — Sun / full illumination

Potential domain:
- flagship media;
- canonical/full primary expression (often long-form, but not defined by duration);
- main episodes;
- definitive explainer;
- hero asset;
- searchable evergreen authority;
- highest-context version of an idea.

Helios is not "YouTube."
A Helios artifact may live on any surface.

Contract idea:

    CANONICAL IDEA -> FULL PRIMARY MEDIA EXPRESSION

Possible future Book trigger:
- distinct runtime/pipeline;
- multiple properties;
- special quality/evidence law;
- independent long-form economics/operations.

Law:
> **HELIOS CARRIES THE FULL LIGHT.**

## EOS — Dawn / first light

Potential domain:
- discovery;
- hooks;
- Shorts;
- Reels;
- TikTok;
- trailers;
- teasers;
- clip derivatives;
- launch variants;
- first-contact experiments.

Eos should route honestly toward deeper value when deeper value exists.

Eos is the natural surface for Theia Clipping outputs, but not every Eos artifact must be a clip.

Possible metrics:
- stop rate;
- first seconds;
- completion;
- share;
- follow;
- route-to-Helios/owned asset.

Law:
> **EOS EARNS THE FIRST LOOK; SHE DOES NOT FAKE THE SUNRISE.**

## SELENE — Moon / cycle / continuity

Potential domain:
- serialized continuity;
- recurring character worlds;
- episodic return;
- calm/bedtime/reflective media;
- playlists/cycles;
- evergreen resurfacing;
- gentle companion media;
- long-term continuity signals.

Selene does **not** replace Mnemosyne or Theia's archive/provenance systems. She names the audience-facing cycle/return function, not canonical storage.

Selene is especially relevant to Pemberton because character affection and return can matter more than one-off utility.

Possible metrics:
- returning viewers;
- episode-to-episode return;
- repeat completion;
- character affinity;
- binge/playlist progression;
- requests for next adventure.

Law:
> **SELENE TURNS A VIEW INTO A RHYTHM OF RETURN.**

## CHILD GOVERNANCE

Do not create separate services just to fit mythology.

The children are not a second routing taxonomy beside Arms:
- Arms describe **how work is transformed**;
- children describe **what role the resulting media plays for the audience**.

Selene is intentionally orthogonal to Helios/Eos: continuity/return can apply to either full or discovery media.

A child earns a separate Book/runtime only when:
- its production contract is materially distinct;
- it has multiple consumers/properties;
- it requires separate policy/telemetry;
- separation reduces complexity;
- evidence supports the operational boundary.

Until then these are Theia foundations.
