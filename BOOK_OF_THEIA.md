# BOOK OF THEIA

**Titan of Visual Media, Video Production, Creative Media Systems, Character/IP Media, and Media Automation**

**Status:** CANONICAL STRATEGIC TITAN — doctrine and boundaries are active; runtime implementation is intentionally incomplete.

Theia is an independent Titan. She is not an Arm of Hyperion, not a media room inside Hyperion, and not a synonym for a video editor.

In mythology Theia is one of the elder Titans, paired with Hyperion and mother of Helios, Selene and Eos. In OTHRYS, the relationship is deliberately useful: **Hyperion owns the economic bet; Theia owns the media machine.**

> **THEIA TURNS IDEAS, KNOWLEDGE, STORIES AND SOURCE MEDIA INTO VISUAL ASSETS THAT CAN BE PRODUCED, VERIFIED, DISTRIBUTED, LEARNED FROM AND EVENTUALLY OPERATED WITH BOUNDED AUTONOMY.**

> **HYPERION ASKS WHETHER THE BET DESERVES OXYGEN. THEIA DECIDES HOW THE BET BECOMES MEDIA.**

## 0. WHY THEIA EXISTS

Media has become its own technical and economic system.

A serious media system now needs:
- source intake;
- rights and provenance;
- research handoff;
- script and narrative structure;
- scene planning;
- character/world continuity;
- visual grammar;
- voice;
- music/SFX;
- asset generation and retrieval;
- subtitles;
- rendering;
- format adaptation;
- clipping;
- packaging;
- human approval;
- publishing;
- platform-specific compliance;
- telemetry;
- retention analysis;
- experiment history;
- localization;
- archive/resurrection;
- cost accounting;
- and an autonomy ladder.

That is too coherent and too important to remain a collection of commercial sub-functions inside Hyperion.

Theia therefore owns a bounded media domain that can later:
1. serve OTHRYS internally;
2. serve many Oroi through stable contracts;
3. expose reusable media Blocks;
4. become a standalone Star or product;
5. operate as a white-label media automation system;
6. or eventually graduate into an independently deployed machine without changing the conceptual contract.

Theia is designed for that future separation from the first day.

## 4. THE HYPERION–THEIA SIBLING CONTRACT

Hyperion and Theia must be close without becoming a blob.

### Hyperion -> Theia

Hyperion supplies a **Media Opportunity Packet** when commercial or portfolio intelligence is relevant.

Minimum fields:
- opportunity ID;
- target audience hypothesis;
- user/audience job;
- intended value;
- property/Oros;
- hypothesis to test;
- candidate surfaces/platforms;
- bounded budget;
- expected decision point;
- commercial optionality;
- known platform dependence;
- rights assumptions;
- trust constraints;
- kill/pivot conditions.

Hyperion may say:
- test this idea;
- test this audience;
- test this format family;
- compare production economics;
- preserve this breakout property;
- reduce cost;
- localize this proven winner;
- prepare a commercial-readiness packet.

Hyperion may **not** prescribe unsafe publication or override Theia's quality/rights gates.

### Theia -> Hyperion

Theia returns a **Media Evidence Packet**:
- production recipe/version;
- source and provenance;
- rights state;
- production cost;
- operator review time;
- rework rate;
- output artifacts;
- platform package;
- publication evidence;
- retention/completion/return metrics;
- audience questions/themes;
- anomaly notes;
- production bottlenecks;
- portability risk;
- recommended next media experiment.

Hyperion then interprets economic value:
- continue;
- increase/decrease oxygen;
- test another audience;
- prepare sponsor/affiliate/product paths;
- bundle;
- license;
- spin out;
- or kill.

> **THEIA DOES NOT CHASE MONEY BY CORRUPTING MEDIA. HYPERION DOES NOT CHASE MEDIA BY BECOMING A STUDIO.**

## 8. ONE CANONICAL IDEA, MANY NATIVE FORMS

Theia inherits and strengthens a crucial law:

> **ONE CANONICAL IDEA MAY HAVE MANY NATIVE FORMS.**

Example:

    VERIFIED KNOWLEDGE OBJECT
      -> 8-minute Helios explainer
      -> 45-second Eos Short
      -> 15-second hook test
      -> carousel
      -> article embed
      -> printable
      -> localized derivative
      -> future book/game/app seed

The derivatives inherit:
- source identity;
- factual claims;
- rights state;
- provenance;
- property identity;
- experiment lineage.

They do **not** inherit identical pacing or composition.

Native-format law:
- Short != cropped long-form;
- carousel != still frames from video;
- TikTok != automatic YouTube mirror;
- children's explainer != adult explainer with simpler words;
- localization != literal translation;
- clip != arbitrary excerpt.

## 9. SOURCE AND RIGHTS LAW

Every publishable artifact must have a rights state.

Source classes:
- OTHRYS-owned;
- operator-owned;
- client-owned with explicit production rights;
- campaign-licensed;
- public domain;
- openly licensed with compatible terms;
- commercially licensed;
- generated/commissioned with recorded rights;
- unknown / blocked.

Minimum provenance:
- source URI or internal ID;
- source owner;
- licence/permission basis;
- retrieval/receipt date;
- transformations;
- asset-level rights for images/music/voice/fonts;
- commercial-use state;
- attribution requirements;
- territory/time restrictions where material.

Unknown material is not silently treated as permission.

> **POPULARITY IS NEVER A LICENCE.**

For clipping:
- captions + crop do not create ownership;
- downloading public media does not create republishing rights;
- campaign/client permission must be retained as evidence.

For original properties:
- character sheets;
- art prompts/models;
- generated/commissioned assets;
- voices;
- music;
- fonts;
- names/trademark searches when warranted;
- and derivative lineage
must be kept sufficiently clean for future licensing or sale.

## 14. HOOKS WITHOUT DECEPTION

Theia may optimize attention aggressively while remaining truthful.

Good hooks:
- real information gap;
- recognizable problem;
- surprising fact;
- strong image;
- genuine consequence;
- narrative tension;
- unresolved practical question.

Bad hooks:

- invent danger;
- manufacture outrage;
- impersonate authority;
- falsify before/after results;
- fabricate facts;
- misrepresent synthetic media as real events where disclosure is required.

## 15. PRODUCTION ECONOMICS

Every property and format needs a measurable cost model.

Track:
- research cost;
- script/model/API cost;
- asset-generation cost;
- voice/audio cost;
- rendering/compute cost;
- storage/bandwidth where material;
- human review time;
- correction/rework;
- publishing/management time;
- cost per finished minute;
- cost per finished artifact;
- cost per approved artifact;
- cost per qualified viewer/action where meaningful;
- derivative marginal cost;
- localization marginal cost.

Theia searches for **minimum sufficient quality**, not minimum possible quality.

Cheap production that destroys audience value is expensive.
Premium production without demand evidence is an unproven bet.

Operator attention is a first-class production cost.

Platforms are habitats, not identical upload sockets.

## 18. TELEMETRY — MEASURE THE RIGHT THING

Raw views are weak evidence.

Depending on format:
- impressions;
- CTR/stop rate;
- first-seconds retention;
- average view duration;
- completion;
- rewatch;
- saves;
- shares;
- meaningful comments;
- subscriber/follower conversion;
- returning viewers;
- search discovery;
- episode-to-episode return;
- owned-surface migration;
- downstream product activation;
- production cost;
- review time;
- correction rate;
- moderation burden;
- rights/policy incidents.

Signal ladder:

    IMPRESSION
      -> STOP / CLICK
      -> WATCH
      -> COMPLETE
      -> REWATCH
      -> SAVE
      -> SHARE
      -> FOLLOW
      -> RETURN
      -> SEEK MORE
      -> CROSS TO OWNED ASSET
      -> PAY / REQUEST / DEPEND

Not every property needs the last rung.
Mipi may prove value through return/affection/series demand before any transaction.
A practical explainer may prove value through search completion and successful task outcomes.

## 20. WINNER DETECTION AND SCALE

A media winner shows repeatability, not just a spike.

Signals:
- performance across multiple artifacts;
- returning audience;
- organic sharing;
- stable/improving retention;
- audience requests for more;
- low enough production burden;
- derivative formats that also work;
- search/evergreen persistence;
- identifiable character/IP affinity;
- owned-surface migration;
- localization potential;
- product/sponsor/licensing intent where relevant.

When a winner appears:

    FREEZE EVIDENCE
      -> PRESERVE IP
      -> EXPLAIN WHY
      -> HARDEN PIPELINE
      -> INCREASE QUALITY WHERE JUSTIFIED
      -> TEST ADJACENCIES
      -> BUILD OWNED SURFACE
      -> RETURN ECONOMIC EVIDENCE TO HYPERION

Theia may recommend scale.
Hyperion allocates economic oxygen.
Governance retains authority.

## 21. KILL, PIVOT, ARCHIVE

Pause/kill when:
- repeated bounded tests produce no meaningful signal;
- production burden exceeds plausible value;
- rights risk is disproportionate;
- platform dependence is unacceptable;
- quality cannot be achieved economically;
- audience fit is wrong;
- moderation burden dominates;
- a stronger experiment deserves resources.

Archive:
- research;
- scripts;
- format genome;
- production recipe;
- character assets;
- prompts;
- cost history;
- metrics;
- lessons;
- reason for kill/pivot.

> **KILL THE BET. KEEP THE LEARNING.**

## 24. AUTONOMY LADDER

Theia must earn autonomy per property and workflow.

    LEVEL 0 — RESEARCH / DRAFT ONLY
    LEVEL 1 — OPERATOR APPROVES EVERY ARTIFACT
    LEVEL 2 — APPROVED LOW-RISK TEMPLATE WITH HUMAN REVIEW
    LEVEL 3 — BOUNDED AUTO-PRODUCTION, HUMAN PUBLISH APPROVAL
    LEVEL 4 — BOUNDED LOW-RISK AUTO-PUBLISH
    LEVEL 5 — EXCEPTION-BASED REVIEW

Higher-risk content stays lower:
- children;
- health/safety;
- breaking news;
- sensitive history;
- legal/financial claims;
- flagship brand/IP.

Autonomy can be revoked automatically when:
- error rate rises;
- policy changes;
- rights state becomes stale;
- quality drops;
- unexplained anomalies occur.

> **AUTONOMY IS EARNED BY EVIDENCE, NOT ENABLED BY CONFIDENCE.**

## 34. CURRENT RESEARCH BASIS — 2026-09-18

Durable observations:
- YouTube monetization requires original/authentic value and scrutinizes generic, repetitive or mass-produced output.
- Recurring characters and formats can be compatible with monetization when episodes materially differ and provide real value.
- YouTube explicitly expects commercial rights to visual/audio elements.
- TikTok Creator Rewards, where available, requires original high-quality videos longer than one minute for qualifying reward content.
- Automation/templates are therefore not the enemy; interchangeable content is.

These are dated external rules. Platform adapters must refresh them before relying on them.

Tool observations:
- programmatic video (for example Remotion-class systems) is attractive for deterministic repeatable visual grammar;
- cartoon SaaS (for example Vyond-class systems) is attractive for early proof before custom infrastructure;
- modern TTS can provide timing useful for captions/animation;
- generative video is valuable as a scene generator but currently should not be assumed to provide stable character continuity or deterministic diagrams;
- clipping SaaS is useful for proving economics before local replacement.
